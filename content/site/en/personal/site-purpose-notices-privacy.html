Purpose of This Site

Last updated: September 13, 2026.

This site is my personal archive and a project I intend to keep developing over the years. Its main purpose is for my son, Sebastián, to get to know me when he is older through my own words and the things I have studied, learned, and created: what has mattered to me, what I have worked on, and how I have understood the world. It is dedicated to him, to his mother, Zurema, to my parents, Raúl and Margarita, and to my brother, Isaías.

I keep it publicly accessible so that anyone interested can read and use the scientific, technical, musical, and educational material I collect here. It is not a university publication and does not offer medical, legal, financial, or professional consulting services.

The content covers my personal life, music, professional career, and favorite video games, alongside scientific models and material on physics, mathematics, computing, and other subjects, including fluid mechanics and Navier–Stokes. In My Theory, I develop my interpretation of time, infinity, quantum theory, and consciousness.

I distinguish between established physics, open research questions, and my personal interpretation and metaphysical position. The scientific material is educational; my ideas about reality express my own position and should not be confused with scientific consensus.

Site Version

I started this website project at 25 and have rebuilt it from earlier versions. The current structure helps me organize the content and update its sections more easily.

Scientific and Educational Disclaimer

I write the scientific material for educational purposes. I use sources, equations, and references, but it may contain errors. Do not rely on this site as the only source for academic work, engineering decisions, medical decisions, legal decisions, or financial decisions. Verify major claims against primary sources, textbooks, professors, or qualified professionals.

Interactive simulations and visual models are educational approximations. They are designed to make ideas easier to understand, not to replace specialized numerical tools, laboratory instruments, or professional scientific software.

Personal and Metaphysical Disclaimer

My writing on Everettian reality, unified duality, finite configurations, ultimate totality, and consciousness-first reality expresses my personal interpretation. Parts of it draw on physics, but that does not establish my metaphysical position.

When I discuss consciousness, identity, death, meaning, or the total structure of reality, I am not presenting a complete physical theory. I develop an argument of my own and distinguish between what has support from physics and what I propose as my personal ontological position.

Privacy and Visitor Data

The site's code does not include a visitor counter, advertising tracker, login system, contact form, or cookies for tracking visitors. Reading settings are saved in the browser's local storage when it is available.

The site may load third-party resources such as fonts, icons, math rendering scripts, or embedded media. Those providers may receive data from requests sent by the browser. If I introduce visitor analytics, I will seek a privacy-respecting option and update this notice before enabling it.

Security Notes

This is a static website with no user accounts and no database. That reduces the amount of sensitive information the site can collect or expose. The site also uses HTTPS, a Content Security Policy, noopener/noreferrer on external links, and youtube-nocookie.com for embedded YouTube content where applicable.

I cannot guarantee absolute security. If you notice a broken link, suspicious behavior, a reading or usability issue, or a content error, use the listed contact or social links to report it.

Reading and Usability Statement

I want the site to be usable with keyboard navigation, screen readers, larger text, reduced motion, higher contrast, visible links, and a readable font mode. The Reading Settings panel lets visitors adapt the reading experience to their needs while preserving the site's overall design.

I will keep reviewing readability and usability as the content, devices, and browsers change. If you notice a broken link, layout glitch, hard-to-read section, media problem, translation issue, or other content or navigation error, please report it through the contact information or social links available on the site.
