Library and Influences

Favorite Works, Books, Notes, and Papers

The rest of this section contains the books, lecture notes, and technical works that shaped the way I think. Sean Carroll sits at the center because his work gave language and structure to questions I already had about time, quantum mechanics, gravity, and existence. These are my favorite works, and many are in English because those are the versions I originally read. These favorite works include established physics, speculative physics, interpretation, and philosophy-of-mind material; inclusion does not mean equal evidential status.

Ewin Tang and Quantum Computing Papers

  • ▣Ewin Tang, 2023. Quantum Machine Learning Without Any Quantum.

  • ▣Ewin Tang, 2022. Dequantizing Algorithms to Understand Quantum Advantage in Machine Learning.

  • ▣Jeongwan Haah, Robin Kothari, and Ewin Tang, 2021. Optimal Learning of Quantum Hamiltonians from High-Temperature Gibbs States. Core paper for learning local Hamiltonian coefficients from high-temperature Gibbs-state samples.
  • ▣Haah, Kothari, and Tang, 2024. Learning Quantum Hamiltonians from High-Temperature Gibbs States and Real-Time Evolutions. Nature Physics article page connecting Gibbs-state learning and real-time evolution.
  • ▣Anshu, Arunachalam, Kuwahara, and Soleimanifar, 2021. Sample-Efficient Learning of Interacting Quantum Systems. Important related paper on local Hamiltonian learning from Gibbs states.
  • ▣Ainesh Bakshi, Allen Liu, Ankur Moitra, and Ewin Tang, 2023. Learning Quantum Hamiltonians at Any Temperature in Polynomial Time. Extends the Hamiltonian-learning program beyond the high-temperature setting.
  • ▣Ainesh Bakshi, Allen Liu, Ankur Moitra, and Ewin Tang, 2024. High-Temperature Gibbs States Are Unentangled and Efficiently Preparable. Useful for understanding when thermal quantum states lose entanglement and become efficiently preparable.
  • ▣Ainesh Bakshi, Allen Liu, Ankur Moitra, and Ewin Tang, 2024. Structure Learning of Hamiltonians from Real-Time Evolution. Studies recovering Hamiltonian interaction structure from dynamics rather than thermal samples.
  • ▣Ainesh Bakshi, John Bostanci, William Kretschmer, Zeph Landau, Jerry Li, Allen Liu, Ryan O'Donnell, and Ewin Tang, 2024. Learning the Closest Product State. Hilbert-space state-learning paper about approximating a state by the closest unentangled product structure.
  • ▣Angelos Pelecanos, Xinyu Tan, Ewin Tang, and John Wright, 2025. Beating Full State Tomography for Unentangled Spectrum Estimation. State-learning paper focused on spectral information without paying the full tomography cost.
  • ▣Ewin Tang and John Wright, 2025. Amplitude Amplification and Estimation Require Inverses.
  • ▣Ewin Tang and John Wright, 2025. Are Controlled Unitaries Helpful?
  • ▣Ainesh Bakshi, Allen Liu, Ankur Moitra, and Ewin Tang, 2025. A Dobrushin Condition for Quantum Markov Chains: Rapid Mixing and Conditional Mutual Information at High Temperature. Further high-temperature Gibbs/Markov-chain direction connected to rapid mixing and conditional mutual information.
  • ▣Francisco Escudero Gutiérrez, Ewin Tang, and Arthur Weggemans, 2025. Conjugate Queries Can Help Estimate a Unitary.
  • ▣Angelos Pelecanos, Jack Spilecki, Ewin Tang, and John Wright, 2025. Mixed State Tomography Reduces to Pure State Tomography. Directly relevant to Hilbert-space state learning because mixed states are density matrices, not just vectors.
