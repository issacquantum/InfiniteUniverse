Erik P. Verlinde

Conformal field theory, holographic entropy, and proposals for emergent gravity, followed by work on black-hole information and quantum spacetime fluctuations. The emergent-gravity and interferometer proposals remain distinct from established results in conformal field theory.

Conformal field theory and strings

  • ▣Erik Verlinde. Fusion rules and modular transformations in 2D conformal field theory. 1988. Research article. Relates fusion coefficients to modular transformations in rational conformal field theory, establishing the formula that bears Verlinde's name.
  • ▣R. Dijkgraaf, E. Verlinde and H. Verlinde. Matrix String Theory. 1997. Research article. Develops the matrix-string description of type IIA strings through two-dimensional supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory, including string interactions and brane states.

Holography and emergent gravity

  • ▣Erik Verlinde. On the Holographic Principle in a Radiation Dominated Universe. 2000. Preprint. Proposes a Cardy-type entropy relation for a closed, radiation-dominated universe and connects its saturation to the Friedmann equation under specified holographic assumptions.
  • ▣Erik P. Verlinde. On the Origin of Gravity and the Laws of Newton. 2010 preprint; 2011 publication. Research article. Proposes an entropic account of gravity based on emergent space and information. It is a theoretical proposal, not an experimentally established origin of gravity.
  • ▣Erik P. Verlinde. Emergent Gravity and the Dark Universe. 2016 preprint; 2017 publication. Research article. Extends the emergent-gravity proposal to de Sitter space and apparent dark-matter effects. Its phenomenological claims depend on the proposed entropy and elastic-response assumptions.
  • ▣Sam van Leuven, Erik P. Verlinde and Manus R. Visser. Towards non-AdS Holography via the Long String Phenomenon. 2018. Research article. Uses long-string degrees of freedom to explore holographic descriptions beyond asymptotically anti-de Sitter settings, including Minkowski and de Sitter geometries.

Black holes, information, and de Sitter space

  • ▣Erik Verlinde and Herman Verlinde. Black Hole Entanglement and Quantum Error Correction. 2012 preprint; 2013 publication. Research article. Applies quantum error correction to the black-hole interior and firewall problem, proposing how interior information could be protected in an entangled system.
  • ▣Erik P. Verlinde and Manus R. Visser. Black hole entropy and long strings. 2022. Essay. Connects black-hole entropy with long-string degrees of freedom and the scaling of their excitation energies, extending the long-string holography program.
  • ▣Joshua Kames-King, Evita Verheijden and Erik Verlinde. No Page Curves for the de Sitter Horizon. 2021 preprint; 2022 publication. Research article. Studies radiation entropy for de Sitter observers in a reduced gravitational model; backreaction obstructs the proposed unitary evaporation at the Page time.
  • ▣Andrew Svesko, Evita Verheijden, Erik P. Verlinde and Manus R. Visser. Quasi-local energy and microcanonical entropy in two-dimensional nearly de Sitter gravity. 2022. Research article. Studies quasi-local thermodynamics and generalized entropy in nearly de Sitter Jackiw-Teitelboim gravity. The linked arXiv record includes the later correction to the original analysis.
  • ▣Alexander Altland, Boris Post, Julian Sonner, Jeremy van der Heijden and Erik Verlinde. Quantum chaos in 2D gravity. 2022 preprint; 2023 publication. Research article. Connects spectral correlations and quantum chaos in two-dimensional gravity with a nonperturbative universe-field-theory description, adding a statistical perspective to holography.
  • ▣Jeremy van der Heijden and Erik Verlinde. An Operator Algebraic Approach To Black Hole Information. 2024 preprint; 2025 publication. Research article. Formulates black-hole information recovery using operator algebras, subfactors, and an entanglement-swap protocol. It is a mathematical framework for the information problem.

Spacetime fluctuations

  • ▣Erik Verlinde and Kathryn M. Zurek. Spacetime Fluctuations in AdS/CFT. 2019 preprint; 2020 publication. Research article. Computes modular-energy fluctuations in an AdS Rindler wedge and examines their possible geometric and light-travel-time effects within AdS/CFT.
  • ▣Erik P. Verlinde and Kathryn M. Zurek. Observational Signatures of Quantum Gravity in Interferometers. 2019 preprint; 2021 publication. Research article. Proposes interferometer signatures of a particular model of quantum spacetime fluctuations. The predicted signal is not a reported detection of quantum gravity.

Edward Witten

Research connecting supersymmetry, geometry, quantum field theory, and strings, with one review of quantum entanglement. Exact results within a specified mathematical or physical model do not by themselves establish that model as a description of nature.

Supersymmetry, geometry, and topology

  • ▣Edward Witten. A new proof of the positive energy theorem. 1981. Research article. Gives a spinorial proof of positive energy in general relativity under the theorem's hypotheses, linking geometric analysis to gravitational stability.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Supersymmetry and Morse theory. 1982. Research article. Uses supersymmetric quantum mechanics to derive Morse-theoretic results, a defining bridge between quantum mechanics and differential topology.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Constraints on supersymmetry breaking. 1982. Research article. Develops index-based, nonperturbative constraints on spontaneous supersymmetry breaking, explaining what can be inferred without a perturbative vacuum analysis.
  • ▣Luis Alvarez-Gaumé and Edward Witten. Gravitational anomalies. 1984. Research article. Calculates gravitational anomalies in chiral quantum theories, identifying quantum consistency conditions that constrain candidate field and string theories.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Topological quantum field theory. 1988. Research article. Constructs a twisted supersymmetric gauge theory whose observables encode Donaldson invariants, giving four-manifold topology a quantum-field-theoretic formulation.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Quantum field theory and the Jones polynomial. 1989. Research article. Relates Chern-Simons quantum field theory to the Jones polynomial and three-manifold invariants, providing a physical construction of knot invariants.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Phases of N=2 Theories In Two Dimensions. 1993. Research article. Uses gauged linear sigma models to connect Calabi-Yau and Landau-Ginzburg phases, making transitions between different descriptions of string compactification tractable.

Seiberg-Witten theory

  • ▣N. Seiberg and E. Witten. Electric-magnetic duality, monopole condensation, and confinement in N=2 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory. 1994. Research article. Determines the low-energy dynamics of pure N=2 supersymmetric Yang-Mills theory using electric-magnetic duality; monopole condensation explains confinement after a supersymmetry-breaking deformation.
  • ▣N. Seiberg and E. Witten. Monopoles, Duality and Chiral Symmetry Breaking in N=2 Supersymmetric QCD. 1994. Research article. Extends the exact-duality analysis to theories with matter, relating singularities in moduli space to monopoles, flavor symmetry, and chiral symmetry breaking.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Monopoles and Four-Manifolds. 1994. Research article. Introduces monopole equations for smooth four-manifolds through supersymmetric gauge theory, yielding the Seiberg-Witten invariants and a new approach to Donaldson theory.

Strings, branes, and M-theory

  • ▣Edward Witten. String Theory Dynamics In Various Dimensions. 1995. Research article. Analyzes strong-coupling limits and dualities of string theories in different dimensions, central to the emergence of the M-theory picture.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Bound States Of Strings And p-Branes. 1995 preprint; 1996 publication. Research article. Studies supersymmetric bound states of strings and D-branes through world-volume gauge dynamics, testing the spectrum required by string dualities.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Small Instantons in String Theory. 1995 preprint; 1996 publication. Research article. Shows how shrinking heterotic instantons generate additional nonperturbative degrees of freedom, including enhanced gauge symmetry, beyond a purely geometric description.
  • ▣Petr Horava and Edward Witten. Heterotic and Type I String Dynamics from Eleven Dimensions. 1995 preprint; 1996 publication. Research article. Proposes the eleven-dimensional orbifold description of the strongly coupled E8×E8 heterotic string, a defining connection between heterotic theory and M-theory.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Solutions Of Four-Dimensional Field Theories Via M Theory. 1997. Research article. Uses brane configurations and their M-theory lift to solve classes of four-dimensional supersymmetric gauge theories, connecting gauge dynamics with geometry.
  • ▣Edward Witten. D-Branes And K-Theory. 1998. Research article. Argues that D-brane charges are classified by spacetime K-theory, incorporating brane-antibrane processes that ordinary cohomology does not fully capture.
  • ▣Nathan Seiberg and Edward Witten. String Theory and Noncommutative Geometry. 1999. Research article. Derives a limit of string theory described by noncommutative gauge fields and the map relating them to ordinary gauge fields.

Holography, amplitudes, and entanglement

  • ▣Edward Witten. Anti De Sitter Space And Holography. 1998. Research article. Formulates the relation between bulk fields in anti-de Sitter space and boundary conformal-field-theory correlation functions, foundational to the AdS/CFT dictionary.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Anti-de Sitter Space, Thermal Phase Transition, And Confinement In Gauge Theories. 1998. Research article. Connects AdS black-hole thermodynamics and the Hawking-Page transition to thermal behavior and confinement in boundary gauge theories.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Perturbative Gauge Theory As A String Theory In Twistor Space. 2003 preprint; 2004 publication. Research article. Recasts gauge-theory scattering amplitudes in twistor space and proposes a topological-string description, a major starting point for modern amplitude methods.
  • ▣Edward Witten. Notes on Some Entanglement Properties of Quantum Field Theory. 2018. Review. Reviews entanglement in quantum field theory through observable algebras and modular theory. This is an expository review, not a claim to originate all the results discussed.

Brian R. Greene

Research on mirror symmetry, topology change, branes, and early-universe models, followed by lecture notes on Calabi-Yau compactification. The cosmological papers explore specific theoretical scenarios rather than established accounts of cosmic history.

Mirror symmetry and topology change

  • ▣B.R. Greene and M.R. Plesser. Duality in Calabi-Yau moduli space. 1990. Research article. Constructs mirror pairs with distinct Calabi-Yau topology but equivalent conformal theories, establishing a concrete foundation for mirror symmetry.
  • ▣Paul S. Aspinwall, Brian R. Greene and David R. Morrison. Multiple Mirror Manifolds and Topology Change in String Theory. 1993. Research article. Uses mirror symmetry to show how string theory connects geometries related by flop transitions, challenging a fixed classical topology.
  • ▣P. S. Aspinwall, B. R. Greene and D. R. Morrison. Calabi-Yau Moduli Space, Mirror Manifolds and Spacetime Topology Change in String Theory. 1993 preprint; 1994 publication. Research article. Develops the detailed moduli-space analysis behind topology-changing transitions and the enlarged set of geometric phases in string theory.
  • ▣Brian R. Greene, David R. Morrison and Andrew Strominger. Black Hole Condensation and the Unification of String Vacua. 1995. Research article. Explains conifold transitions through condensation of charged black-hole states, connecting previously distinct families of Calabi-Yau string vacua.
  • ▣Michael R. Douglas, Brian R. Greene and David R. Morrison. Orbifold Resolution by D-Branes. 1997. Research article. Uses D-brane probes to resolve orbifold singularities and study the resulting vacuum moduli space, linking brane gauge theory to geometry.
  • ▣Brian R. Greene. D-Brane Topology Changing Transitions. 1997 preprint; 1998 publication. Research article. Examines topology-changing transitions using D-brane probes, testing how spacetime geometry depends on the objects used to measure it.

Inflation and extra dimensions

  • ▣Richard Easther, Brian R. Greene, William H. Kinney and Gary Shiu. Inflation as a Probe of Short Distance Physics. 2001. Research article. Studies whether short-distance physics could leave imprints in primordial perturbations during inflation, with predictions dependent on the assumed ultraviolet model.
  • ▣Brian R. Greene and Janna Levin. Dark Energy and Stabilization of Extra Dimensions. 2007. Research article. Explores Casimir energies as a possible mechanism for stabilizing extra dimensions and driving accelerated expansion in a specific brane-world scenario.

String and brane gas cosmology

  • ▣Richard Easther, Brian R. Greene, Mark G. Jackson and Daniel Kabat. Brane gas cosmology in M-theory: late time behavior. 2002 preprint; 2003 publication. Research article. Analyzes late-time expansion in an anisotropic M-theory brane-gas model, showing how wrapping configurations affect the sizes of spatial dimensions.
  • ▣Richard Easther, Brian R. Greene, Mark G. Jackson and Daniel Kabat. Brane gases in the early universe: thermodynamics and cosmology. 2003 preprint; 2004 publication. Research article. Combines brane thermodynamics with Einstein-Boltzmann evolution. Under its allowed initial conditions, branes typically annihilate before freeze-out, limiting dimensional selection.
  • ▣Richard Easther, Brian R. Greene, Mark G. Jackson and Daniel Kabat. String windings in the early universe. 2004 preprint; 2005 publication. Research article. Tests winding-string dynamics numerically and analytically. Three large dimensions can result, but are not statistically favored under the paper's initial-state assumptions.
  • ▣Brian Greene, Daniel Kabat and Stefanos Marnerides. Bouncing and cyclic string gas cosmologies. 2008 preprint; 2009 publication. Research article. Builds bouncing and cyclic string-gas models by modifying the effective gravitational action; nonsingular evolution is a property of those models, not an observed cosmic history.
  • ▣Brian Greene, Dan Kabat and Stefanos Marnerides. Dynamical Decompactification and Three Large Dimensions. 2009 preprint; 2010 publication. Research article. Revisits the Brandenberger-Vafa mechanism using impact-parameter-dependent winding interactions, identifying a regime in which three large dimensions can be dynamically selected.

Lecture notes

  • ▣Brian Greene. String Theory on Calabi-Yau Manifolds. 1997. Lecture notes. TASI-96 lectures on compactification and quantum geometry, including mirror symmetry and topology change. They provide a longer pedagogical guide to the research above.
