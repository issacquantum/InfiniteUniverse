How I Started Playing Tekken

Tekken is one of my favourite games, and I have been playing it for most of my life.

It all started around age five with Tekken 2. What drew me to Kazuya Mishima was his fighting style, his Devil transformation, and his theme music, the same track playing in the background of this section.

Then came Tekken 3. I chose Jin Kazama, the son of Kazuya and Jun, for his appearance at first. I soon loved his moveset, which combined Kazuya's aggression with Jun's defensive style. The 3D movement gave me more ways to play. I spent so much time with it that I memorized the full move list of every character, including secret moves.

Kazuya has been my main throughout the series, except in Tekken 3, where he was absent and I played Jin instead. I liked that Jin shared so much of his fighting style. Kazuya has gained new moves over the years without losing what I liked about him, and I still enjoy learning to play him in Tekken 8. He is demanding: playing him well takes precision, execution, and practice. I love his design and his story. He is my favourite video game character of all time.

Tekken has been part of my life for years, and I take it seriously. I enjoy improving my execution, learning how to punish mistakes, and figuring out how to adapt to each opponent.

My favourite entries are Tekken 3, Tekken Tag Tournament, Tekken Tag Tournament 2, Tekken 7, and Tekken 8. I entered competitive online play with Tekken 7 and I'm still active in Tekken 8, though life keeps me busy between music, physics, programming, technical work, and more.

Gran Turismo and Other Games

I love Gran Turismo. It has been part of my life since childhood, and I still spend a great deal of time playing it.

Alongside Tekken, it is one of the games I remember playing when I was around five or six. I still enjoy returning to it after all these years.

I don't play many games. Tekken and Gran Turismo are the two that have mattered most to me. I also play Grand Theft Auto V and Dragon Ball: Sparking Zero simply for fun.

Gran Turismo 7 is the racing game I take most seriously. I have spent a huge amount of time building my car collection; collecting the cars is part of the enjoyment for me. I own many duplicate Porsches simply because I like them that much.

Gran Turismo 7 is also my father's favourite video game. Sharing that interest with him is one of the things that makes it special to me.

My favourite car manufacturer in Gran Turismo 7 is Porsche, and I have a particular attachment to the Porsche 911 GT3 RS. I have never driven a real car, so all my experience behind the wheel has been in the game.

Porsche is also my favourite car manufacturer in real life. One of my dreams is to own at least a Porsche Macan GTS one day. I admire the engineering behind these cars, and I love being able to drive them in Gran Turismo 7.

My favourite circuit is the Nürburgring Nordschleife. It is extremely long, difficult, and narrow, with elevation changes and blind corners that keep me tense throughout a lap in Gran Turismo 7. Of all the circuits in Gran Turismo 7, it is the one I keep coming back to.

The Nordschleife is the only track that has ever fully held my attention. It feels unpredictable, and no matter how many laps I do, it never feels repetitive. I never get tired of it.

If my financial circumstances had been different, I would have tried to become a professional driver. I would still have wanted to study physics, play music, and program, but I would have wanted to race too. My goal would have been to drive for Porsche.
