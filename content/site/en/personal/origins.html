I was born in Mexico City and grew up in a very small room in a vecindad, a shared housing complex. That room was our entire home. My father taught me to read when I was very young.

When I was around five or six, my mother set me a challenge: read part of a book and explain what I had understood. I do not remember the title or the pages I read. I also had a cleaning chore to do with my brother. If I finished the chore and explained the reading correctly, the reward would be a PlayStation. That was how I got my first PlayStation 1.

That was where my obsession with Tekken and Gran Turismo began, and it has stayed with me ever since. I enjoyed spending time with them and improving my play. Tekken was where I started taking competition seriously at that age.

My childhood had difficult periods, with problems at home and financial limitations. When I was around six or seven, things changed at home, and it was difficult for me. Later, financial problems and responsibilities also affected my education. I had to interrupt my studies, even though I still wanted to learn.

Despite all of that, my parents have been the biggest influence on my life in many ways. They are both very intelligent, and I have always admired how easily they understand how things work. They know how to take apart and repair engines, vehicles, motorcycles, electrical appliances, and many other things.
