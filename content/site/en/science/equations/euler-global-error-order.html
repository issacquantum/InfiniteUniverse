Euler Global Error Order

Equation

$$ \|e_N\|=O(h) $$

Literal Reading

The norm of e subscript N is big O of h.

Common Reading

If the step size is cut in half, the final Euler error should roughly cut in half for a smooth stable problem.

Symbols

\(\|e_N\|\): magnitude of the final accumulated error.

\(O(h)\): first-order scaling with step size.

\(h\): numerical time step.

Meaning of the Equation

The global error order applies for smooth stable problems.

Conditions and units

\(e_N=y_N-y(T)\) is the error after \(N\) steps over a fixed interval, with \(Nh=T-t_0\). Its units are those of the state, while \(h\) has time units; the bound’s constant carries the remaining units. Big O is an asymptotic bound as \(h\to0\), not an exact halving rule at every step size. Stability restrictions still apply.
