Born Rule and POVM (positive operator-valued measure) Measurement

Equation

$$ E_m=M_m^\dagger M_m,\qquad \sum_m E_m=I $$ $$ P(m)=\operatorname{Tr}(E_m\rho),\qquad P(m)=\langle\psi|E_m|\psi\rangle\ \text{for a pure state} $$

Literal Reading

E sub m equals M sub m dagger M sub m, and the sum over m of E sub m equals I. P of m equals the trace of E sub m rho; for a pure state, P of m equals bra psi E sub m ket psi.

Common Reading

A POVM effect turns a quantum state into the probability of a measurement outcome.

Symbols

\(P(m)\): probability of measurement result \(m\).

\(|\psi\rangle\): the state before measurement.

\(M_m\): the measurement operator for outcome \(m\).

\(M_m^\dagger\): the Hermitian adjoint of \(M_m\).

\(E_m\): a POVM effect, equal to \(M_m^\dagger M_m\) in this representation.

\(I\): the identity operator.

Meaning of the Equation

The effects \(E_m\) are positive operators that add to the identity, so the probabilities of all possible outcomes add to one.

Form used in the article

$$|\psi_m\rangle = \frac{M_m|\psi\rangle}{\sqrt{P(m)}},\qquad \sum_m E_m = I$$

Ket psi subscript m equals M subscript m acting on ket psi, divided by the square root of P of m; the sum of E subscript m over m equals the identity.

\(|\psi_m\rangle\) is the normalized conditional state after outcome \(m\), provided \(P(m)>0\). This formula assumes a pure input and one measurement operator for that outcome. The density operator \(\rho\) describes the input in the general probability formula; \(\operatorname{Tr}\) is the trace. States, effects, probabilities and measurement operators here are dimensionless. A POVM determines probabilities but does not uniquely determine the state update: the measurement instrument is additional information. TU Delft, Measurement operators.

Equivalent notation

$$P(m)=\langle\psi|M_m^\dagger M_m|\psi\rangle$$For a normalized pure input, substitute \(E_m=M_m^\dagger M_m\): P of m equals bra psi M subscript m dagger M subscript m ket psi.
