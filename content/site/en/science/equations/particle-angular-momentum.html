Angular Momentum of a Particle

Equation

$$\mathbf L=\mathbf r\times\mathbf p$$

Literal reading

Vector L equals vector r cross vector p.

Conceptual reading

Angular momentum measures motion about a specified origin.

Symbols

\(\mathbf L\) is angular momentum; \(\mathbf r\) is displacement from the origin; \(\mathbf p\) is linear momentum. The cross is the right-handed vector product; bold symbols denote vectors.

Values, units and domains

Position is in m, momentum in kg·m/s and angular momentum in kg·m²/s. The result depends on the origin and frame.

Meaning

Only momentum perpendicular to the displacement contributes. Radial motion has zero angular momentum about that origin.

Assumptions and limits

Particle description with all vectors evaluated relative to the same origin and frame. A system’s total angular momentum requires summing over its particles.

Example

A particle 2 m along x with momentum 3 kg·m/s along y has angular momentum 6 kg·m²/s along z.
