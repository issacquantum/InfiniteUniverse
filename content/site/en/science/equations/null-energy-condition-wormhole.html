Null Energy Condition Violation

Equation

$$ T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu < 0 $$

Literal Reading

T subscript mu nu times k superscript mu times k superscript nu is less than zero.

Common Reading

The expression shows a violation of the null energy condition in a lightlike direction.

Symbols

\(T_{\mu\nu}\): stress energy tensor.

\(k^\mu\): null vector, meaning a lightlike direction.

\(<0\): negative value for this contraction.

Meaning of the Equation

Traversable Morris-Thorne wormholes require NEC violation near the throat.

Conditions and units

The NEC itself requires \(T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu\ge0\) for every null vector. The displayed negative contraction violates it for at least one direction. Indices run from 0 to 3 and are summed; \(g_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu=0\). With dimensionless tangent normalization and length-valued coordinates, the contraction has energy-density units J/m³. Rescaling the null vector changes its magnitude but not the sign. The throat conclusion assumes the Einstein field equations of classical general relativity; it is not an independent existence proof for a traversable wormhole.
