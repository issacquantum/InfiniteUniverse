Small-Oscillation Angular Frequency

Equation

$$\omega_0=\sqrt{k_{\mathrm{eff}}/m}$$

Literal reading

Omega subscript zero equals the square root of effective stiffness divided by mass.

Conceptual reading

Positive potential curvature and inertia determine the local oscillation rate.

Symbols

\(\omega_0\) is the angular frequency of the linearized undamped motion; subscript 0 labels this natural frequency. \(k_{\mathrm{eff}}\) is the positive second position derivative of potential energy at equilibrium; \(m\) is mass. The square root covers the whole quotient.

Values, units and domains

Stiffness is in N/m, positive mass in kg and frequency in rad/s. Radians are dimensionless in SI. The frequency depends on the system.

Meaning

Different smooth potentials share approximately harmonic motion near a nondegenerate minimum because their leading restoring term is linear.

Assumptions and limits

One-dimensional, undamped Newtonian motion with sufficiently small displacement about a positive-curvature equilibrium. Larger amplitudes can make frequency depend on amplitude.

Example

For effective stiffness 8 N/m and mass 2 kg, the angular frequency is 2 rad/s and the period is π s.
