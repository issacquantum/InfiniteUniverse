Product State Factorization

Equation

$$ |\phi\rangle=|\phi_1\rangle\otimes|\phi_2\rangle\otimes\cdots\otimes|\phi_n\rangle $$

Literal Reading

Ket phi equals the tensor product of ket phi subscript one, ket phi subscript two, and the remaining factors through ket phi subscript n.

Common Reading

A product state is a state with no entanglement between the listed subsystems in the chosen factorization.

Symbols

\(|\phi\rangle\): full system state.

\(|\phi_i\rangle\): state of subsystem \(i\).

\(\otimes\): tensor product, used to combine subsystem Hilbert spaces.

\(\cdots\): continuation through all \(n\) subsystems.

Meaning of the Equation

Factorization means the joint state contains no entanglement between those parts. Measurements on one part do not require updating an entangled partner state.

Conditions and units

The \(n\) subsystem kets are normalized and the tensor-product structure is specified in advance. The full ket is then normalized and dimensionless. This formula concerns a pure product state. A separable mixed state can be a mixture of different product states and can have classical correlations without entanglement.

Worked example or interpretation

For two subsystems in ket 0 and ket 1, the tensor product is ket 01. A basis measurement returns those values with certainty. If each subsystem is instead an equal superposition, the four joint basis outcomes each have probability 1/4, still with no entanglement.

Source

John Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information.
