Lorentz Force Law

Equation

$$\mathbf{F}=q(\mathbf{E}+\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B})$$

Literal Reading

F equals q times the quantity E plus v cross B.

Common Reading

A charge experiences force from the electric field and from motion through the magnetic field.

Symbols

  • \(q\): electric charge.
  • \(\mathbf{E}\): electric field.
  • \(\mathbf{v}\): particle velocity.
  • \(\mathbf{B}\): magnetic field.

Meaning of the Equation

The electric part can change speed directly, while the magnetic part acts sideways on moving charges. That sideways term bends paths without doing ordinary work.

Conditions and units

\(\mathbf F=d\mathbf p/dt\) is the electromagnetic three-force in an inertial frame, in N. Charge \(q\) is in C, \(\mathbf E\) in V/m, \(\mathbf v\) in m/s, and \(\mathbf B\) in tesla. Fields and velocity must be evaluated in the same frame at the particle’s position. The cross product is perpendicular to \(\mathbf v\), so the magnetic contribution to instantaneous power is zero; \(\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf v=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v\). Relativistically, momentum is not simply mass times velocity.

Worked example or interpretation

For positive charge 1 C moving at 2 m/s along the x axis in a magnetic field 3 T along the y axis, with zero electric field, the magnetic force is 6 N along the z axis. Its perpendicular direction follows the right-hand rule; negative charge reverses the force.

Source

David Tong, Electromagnetism.
