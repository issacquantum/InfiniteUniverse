Relativistic Relation Between Energy and Momentum

Equation

$$E^2 = (pc)^2 + (mc^2)^2$$

Literal Reading

E squared equals the square of the product p c plus the square of the product m c squared.

Common Reading

The invariant relation connecting total energy, momentum, and rest mass.

Symbols

\(E\): total relativistic energy.

\(p\): relativistic momentum.

\(m\): rest mass.

\(c\): speed of light.

Meaning of the Equation

Rest mass is the invariant length of the energy-momentum relation. Massless particles satisfy the same relation with zero rest mass, giving E equals pc.

Conditions and units

\(p=|\mathbf p|\) is momentum magnitude in kg m/s, \(m\) invariant mass in kg, \(c\) in m/s, and \(E\) total energy in J, all in one inertial frame. Both sides have units J². For a physical future-directed particle use the positive-energy root. In curved spacetime the same relation holds in a local orthonormal frame; conserved coordinate energies may have different definitions.

Worked example or interpretation

If pc=3 J and mc²=4 J in an illustrative particle state, the positive total energy is √(9+16)=5 J. Its kinetic energy is 5−4=1 J, not 5 J. The constant c is exactly 299792458 m/s.

Source

Sean M. Carroll, Lecture Notes on General Relativity.
