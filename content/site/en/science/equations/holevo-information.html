Holevo Information

Equation

$$ \chi = S\!\left(\sum_x p_x\rho_x\right) - \sum_x p_x S(\rho_x) $$

Literal Reading

χ equals S of sum sub x p sub x rho sub x minus sum sub x p sub x S of rho sub x.

Common Reading

Chi equals the entropy of the average state minus the average entropy of the states.

Symbols

\(\chi\): the Holevo information.

\(p_x\): the probability of preparing state \(\rho_x\).

\(\rho_x\): one possible quantum state in the ensemble.

\(\sum_x p_x\rho_x\): the average quantum state.

\(S\): von Neumann entropy.

Meaning of the Equation

Holevo information upper-bounds accessible classical information from an ensemble. Capacity statements require channel and asymptotic assumptions.

Conditions and units

The ensemble probabilities satisfy \(p_x\ge0\) and \(\sum_xp_x=1\), and each \(\rho_x\) is a normalized density operator. With \(S(\rho)=-\operatorname{Tr}(\rho\log_2\rho)\), \(\chi\) is in bits; natural logarithms give nats. The bound concerns mutual information between preparation label \(x\) and a measurement outcome. It need not be achievable by a single measurement on one copy.

Reference

John Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information.
