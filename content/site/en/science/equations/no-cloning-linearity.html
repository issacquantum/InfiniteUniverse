No Cloning and Linearity

Equation

$$ U\left(|\psi\rangle|0\rangle\right) = |\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle $$

Literal Reading

U applied to the product of ket psi and ket zero equals the product of ket psi with itself.

Common Reading

The kind of operation a perfect universal quantum copier would need to perform.

Symbols

\(U\): a hypothetical unitary operation that would perform the copying.

\(|\psi\rangle\): an arbitrary unknown quantum state.

\(|0\rangle\): a blank target state prepared to receive the copy.

\(|\psi\rangle|\psi\rangle\): two identical copies of the original state.

Meaning of the Equation

If a universal copier worked on arbitrary superpositions, linearity would produce contradictions. Unknown quantum states cannot be copied like classical bit strings.

Conditions and units

The same fixed operation and blank state must work for every normalized input. Unitarity would preserve the overlap of two inputs while cloning would square it, so arbitrary nonorthogonal states cannot both be cloned perfectly. A known orthogonal set can be copied; this does not contradict the theorem. Kets and the unitary operator are dimensionless, and adjacent kets denote a tensor product.
