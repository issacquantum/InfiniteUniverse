Physical Reality as a Law-Compatible Subset

Equation

$$R_{\mathrm{physical}}=\{x\in\Omega\mid E_{\mathrm{physical}}(x)=1,\ L(x)=1\}$$

Literal reading

R subscript physical equals the set of x belonging to capital omega such that E subscript physical of x equals one and L of x equals one. The two predicate conditions are joined by “and.”

Conceptual reading

This personal definition selects physically instantiated forms compatible with physical law from a broader ontology. Law compatibility alone does not establish physical existence.

Symbols

\(R_{\mathrm{physical}}\) names the physical subset; \(x\) is a form; \(\Omega\) is the total domain. The subscript physical labels a mode. \(E_{\mathrm{physical}}(x)\) tests physical instantiation, not energy; \(L(x)\) tests law compatibility, not length or a Lagrangian. The value 1 means true. Braces delimit a set, \(\in\) means membership and \(\mid\) means “such that.” The comma requires both predicates. Equals signs define the set or assert a truth value.

Values, units and domains

The set, labels and predicates have no SI units. No universal numerical value is assigned to a form. The predicate 1 is a truth indicator, not a measurement or probability.

Meaning

Physical reality is narrower than Ultimate Totality. Mathematical or conceptual description is insufficient for inclusion: the form must also be physically instantiated and law-compatible.

Assumptions and limits

This organizes my metaphysical distinctions; it is not an experimental test or a completed mathematical axiomatization. Applying L requires specified physical laws and evidence. The notation does not show that consciousness follows from quantum mechanics or collapses a wavefunction.

Example

A physically unrealized configuration may be allowed by a theory. Compatibility would hold, but physical instantiation would not, so the configuration would not enter this subset. A counterpossible form cannot qualify merely by being described.

Source

Issac Tabares, My Theory.
