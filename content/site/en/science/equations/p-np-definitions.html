P and NP (nondeterministic polynomial time) Definitions

Equation

$$ P=\bigcup_{k\ge1}\mathrm{TIME}(n^k),\qquad NP=\bigcup_{k\ge1}\mathrm{NTIME}(n^k) $$

Literal Reading

P equals the union, over integers k at least one, of TIME of n to the k. N P equals the corresponding union of NTIME of n to the k.

Common Reading

This separates finding a solution from checking one.

Symbols

\(\mathrm{TIME}(n^k)\): deterministic computation bounded by a polynomial.

\(\mathrm{NTIME}(n^k)\): nondeterministic polynomial-time computation.

\(\bigcup_{k\ge1}\): union over all polynomial degrees.

Meaning of the Equation

\(P\subseteq NP\), but whether \(P=NP\) is unresolved.

Conditions and units

\(n\) is the input length in bits and \(k\) a fixed polynomial degree for each algorithm. TIME and NTIME classify decision problems by worst-case deterministic and nondeterministic time bounds, with the usual asymptotic constants. NP also has an equivalent polynomial-time certificate-verification definition for yes instances. These are dimensionless operation counts under a computation model, not physical runtimes, and the notation does not settle P versus NP.
