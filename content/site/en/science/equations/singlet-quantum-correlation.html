Singlet Quantum Correlation

Equation

$$ E_Q(a,b)=-\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}=-\cos\theta_{ab} $$

Literal Reading

E subscript Q of a and b equals minus the dot product of a and b, and also minus the cosine of theta subscript a b.

Common Reading

The spin-singlet correlation for outcomes normalized to plus or minus one, with detector directions represented on the Bloch sphere.

Symbols

\(E_Q(a,b)\): the quantum-mechanical expectation value of the product of the two measurement results.

\(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}\): unit vectors pointing along the two detector directions.

\(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}\): the dot product measuring how aligned the two directions are.

\(\theta_{ab}\): the angle between the two detector directions.

\(-\cos\theta_{ab}\): the singlet anticorrelation law.

Meaning of the Equation

Here \(\theta_{ab}\) is the angle between spin-measurement directions, or between Bloch-sphere measurement axes. For linear photon polarizers whose physical axes differ by \(\alpha-\beta\), the corresponding singlet correlation is \(-\cos[2(\alpha-\beta)]\). Each local outcome remains random; the correlation cannot transmit a controllable signal.
