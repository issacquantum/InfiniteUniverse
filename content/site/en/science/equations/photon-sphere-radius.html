Photon Sphere Radius

Equation

$$r_{\text{ph}} = \frac{3GM}{c^2}$$

Literal Reading

r sub ph equals 3GM over c to the power of 2.

Common Reading

The unstable circular light orbit around a non rotating black hole.

Symbols

\(r_{\text{ph}}\): photon sphere radius.

\(M\): black hole mass.

\(G\): gravitational constant.

\(c\): speed of light.

Meaning of the Equation

For Schwarzschild only, \(r_{\rm ph}=3GM/c^2=\frac{3}{2}r_s\). Kerr photon orbits depend on spin.

Scope and Limits

This radius is for a Schwarzschild black hole. Spin splits the light-orbit structure into direction-dependent photon regions, so real rotating black holes need the Kerr treatment.

Conditions and units

\(r_{\rm ph}\) is an areal radius in m, and \(r_s=2GM/c^2\) is the Schwarzschild horizon radius. With \(M\) in kg, \(G\) in m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻², and \(c\) in m/s, the ratio has length units. It is not the observed angular shadow size or the critical impact parameter; those are different quantities.
