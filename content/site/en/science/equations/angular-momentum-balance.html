Angular Momentum Balance

Equation

$$\frac{d\mathbf L}{dt}=\boldsymbol\tau_{\rm ext}$$

Literal reading

The time derivative of vector L equals external torque vector tau.

Conceptual reading

Net external torque changes total angular momentum about the chosen origin.

Symbols

\(\mathbf L\) is total angular momentum; \(t\) is time; \(d/dt\) differentiates each vector component. \(\boldsymbol\tau_{\rm ext}\) is net external torque, with ext meaning external.

Values, units and domains

Angular momentum is in kg·m²/s, time in s and torque in N·m. Both angular momentum and torque are axial vectors.

Meaning

If net external torque vanishes, total angular momentum is conserved even while internal motion changes.

Assumptions and limits

Use a fixed origin in an inertial frame and a closed system whose internal torques cancel. An accelerating origin or momentum flux requires additional terms.

Example

A constant external torque of 2 N·m along z for 3 s increases the z component of angular momentum by 6 kg·m²/s.
