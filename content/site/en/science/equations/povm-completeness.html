POVM Completeness

Equation

$$\sum_m E_m=I$$

Literal reading

The sum over m of E subscript m equals the identity operator I.

Conceptual reading

The positive effects of a complete measurement account for all possible outcomes.

Symbols

\(m\) is a discrete outcome label; \(E_m\) its positive measurement effect; \(\sum_m\) adds effects over all outcomes; \(I\) is the identity on the input Hilbert space. It is not the imaginary unit i.

Values, units and domains

The effects and identity are dimensionless operators. Completeness is an operator equality, so it holds on every allowed input vector, not just one chosen state.

Meaning

For every normalized input state, completeness makes the Born probabilities sum to one.

Assumptions and limits

Discrete positive-operator-valued measure (POVM); positivity is a separate required condition. Continuous outcomes require an appropriate operator-valued measure instead of this discrete sum.

Example

The two projectors onto |0⟩ and |1⟩ add to the identity of a qubit. Omitting either projector would leave an incomplete outcome list.

Source

John Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information (2018).
