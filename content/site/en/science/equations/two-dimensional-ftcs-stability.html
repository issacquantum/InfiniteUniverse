Two-Dimensional FTCS Heat Stability

Equation

$$\lambda_x+\lambda_y\le\frac12$$

Literal reading

Lambda subscript x plus lambda subscript y is less than or equal to one half.

Conceptual reading

Diffusion in both grid directions shares the same explicit stability budget.

Symbols

\(\lambda_x\) is alpha times the time step divided by the squared x spacing; \(\lambda_y\) uses the y spacing. Alpha is thermal diffusivity. Subscripts x and y label Cartesian directions, not vector components to multiply. The plus adds the two diffusion numbers and \(\le\) includes the limiting value.

Values, units and domains

Both diffusion numbers are dimensionless and nonnegative. Diffusivity is positive in m²/s, time step in s and positive grid spacings in m. Equal spacings give equal diffusion numbers, each at most one quarter.

Meaning

The five-point explicit heat stencil couples four neighbours, so the sum of the two directional diffusion numbers controls the largest grid-mode amplification.

Assumptions and limits

Constant isotropic diffusivity, uniform Cartesian mesh and forward Euler with centred differences, on an infinite or periodic grid. Other boundaries need stable implementation. Anisotropic diffusion and other discretizations require their own analysis.

Example

For alpha = 10⁻⁴ m²/s, both spacings 0.01 m and time step 0.2 s, each diffusion number is 0.2 and the sum 0.4 is stable. A 0.3 s step gives sum 0.6 and fails this condition.

For a Fourier grid mode, the dimensionless amplification per step is \(g=1-4\lambda_x\sin^2(\theta_x/2)-4\lambda_y\sin^2(\theta_y/2)\). Here \(\theta_x=k_x\Delta x\) and \(\theta_y=k_y\Delta y\) are dimensionless phase increments, \(k_x,k_y\) are wave-number components in rad/m, and the positive spacings \(\Delta x,\Delta y\) are in m. Sine squared ranges from zero to one, so the smallest amplification is \(1-4(\lambda_x+\lambda_y)\). Requiring every mode to satisfy \(|g|\le1\) gives the stated bound.
