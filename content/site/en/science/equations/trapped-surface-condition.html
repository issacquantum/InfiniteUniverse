Trapped Surface Condition

Equation

$$\theta_{(\ell)} < 0$$

Literal Reading

Theta subscript ell is less than zero.

Common Reading

Outgoing light rays are converging rather than spreading outward.

Symbols

\(\theta_{(\ell)}\): expansion of outgoing null geodesics.

\(< 0\): negative expansion, meaning the light front is shrinking.

Meaning of the Equation

The displayed inequality gives the outgoing condition only. A closed future-trapped surface requires both future-directed null expansions to be negative: \(\theta_{(\ell)}<0\) and \(\theta_{(n)}<0\), where \(n\) labels the ingoing null normal. One negative expansion alone does not establish a trapped surface.

Scope and Limits

This is a geometric condition on null congruences. It is powerful because it does not depend on a simple escape-speed picture, but applying it in realistic collapse requires the full spacetime geometry.
