Potential near a Stable Equilibrium

Equation

$$U(x)\approx U(x_0)+\tfrac12U^{\prime\prime}(x_0)(x-x_0)^2$$

Literal reading

U of x is approximately U of x zero plus one half U double prime evaluated at x zero times the square of x minus x zero.

Conceptual reading

Near a nondegenerate minimum, a smooth potential is approximately quadratic.

Symbols

\(U\) is potential energy as a function of position \(x\); \(x_0\) is equilibrium position. Primes mean derivatives with respect to x; \(U^{\prime\prime}(x_0)\) is the second derivative evaluated there. The square covers the full displacement. \(\approx\) denotes truncation of a Taylor expansion.

Values, units and domains

Position is in m, U in J and its second derivative in J/m² = N/m. At the equilibrium the first derivative is zero; positive second derivative gives a nondegenerate minimum.

Meaning

The constant value U(x₀) sets the energy reference. The curvature supplies the linear restoring force; the absent linear term reflects equilibrium.

Assumptions and limits

Requires a sufficiently smooth potential, positive curvature and displacement small enough that higher Taylor terms are negligible. A flat minimum with zero curvature needs higher-order terms.

Example

With U(x₀) = 0 J, curvature 8 N/m and displacement 0.1 m, the quadratic energy is 0.5 × 8 × 0.01 = 0.04 J.
