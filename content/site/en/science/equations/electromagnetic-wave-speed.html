Electromagnetic Wave Speed

Equation

$$c=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}}$$

Literal Reading

c equals 1 over square root of mu 0 times epsilon 0.

Common Reading

In vacuum, the electric and magnetic constants combine to give the speed of electromagnetic waves.

Symbols

  • \(c\): speed of light in vacuum.
  • \(\mu_0\): vacuum permeability.
  • \(\epsilon_0\): vacuum permittivity.

Meaning of the Equation

This relation is for electromagnetic waves in vacuum: \(c=1/\sqrt{\mu_0\epsilon_0}\). In media, wave speed changes.

Conditions and units

In SI, \(c=299792458\) m/s exactly, \(\epsilon_0\) has units F/m and \(\mu_0\) H/m. Their product has units s²/m². Since the 2019 SI revision, \(\mu_0\) and \(\epsilon_0\) individually have measurement uncertainty; \(c\) remains exact. A material’s dispersion and response determine its phase and group speeds.
