Neural Network Layer

Equation

$$ h=\sigma(Wx+b) $$

Literal Reading

h equals sigma applied to the sum of W times x and b.

Common Reading

This is a learned transformation followed by a nonlinear response, allowing the model to represent patterns beyond a single linear map.

Symbols

\(x\): input vector.

\(W\): learned weight matrix.

\(b\): learned bias vector.

\(\sigma\): nonlinear activation function.

\(h\): output representation of the layer.

Meaning of the Equation

The layer first mixes inputs with learned weights, then bends the result through a nonlinear function. That nonlinearity is what lets stacked layers represent patterns that a single linear formula cannot.

Conditions and units

For \(n\) inputs and \(m\) outputs, \(W\) is an \(m\)-by-\(n\) matrix and \(b,h\) have \(m\) components. The usual pointwise activation applies \(\sigma\) to each component of \(Wx+b\). Model inputs are often nondimensionalized; otherwise weights and bias must make each sum dimensionally compatible. The equation specifies the forward map, not the training algorithm or an accuracy guarantee.
