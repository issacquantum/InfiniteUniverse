QFT (quantum field theory) Microcausality

Equation

$$[\phi(x),\phi(y)] = 0\qquad \text{for spacelike separation}$$

Literal Reading

open bracket phi of x comma phi of y close bracket equals 0 for spacelike separation.

Common Reading

The local causality condition of relativistic quantum field theory.

Symbols

\(\phi(x)\): field operator at spacetime point \(x\).

\([\ ,\ ]\): commutator.

Spacelike separation: events outside each other's light cones.

Meaning of the Equation

For spacelike separation, bosonic field commutators vanish. For fermionic fields, the corresponding locality condition uses anticommutators.

Conditions and units

The commutator is \([A,B]=AB-BA\); zero means the two operator orderings agree, not that either field vanishes. \(x,y\) denote spacetime points. For a scalar field in four dimensions with \(\hbar=c=1\), the field has mass dimension one and the commutator dimension two. Field operators are distributions, so rigorous statements use suitably smeared fields. Local observable commutativity rules out signalling by spacelike separated local operations while still allowing correlations.
