Leaky Integrate-and-Fire Neuron

Equation

$$\tau_m\frac{dV}{dt}=-(V-E_L)+RI(t)$$

Literal Reading

Tau subscript m times the time derivative of V equals minus the difference between V and E subscript L, plus R times I of t.

Common Reading

This is a model of neural excitability and spiking threshold, not a complete biological neuron and not a theory of consciousness.

Symbols

  • \(V\): membrane voltage.
  • \(\tau_m\): membrane time constant.
  • \(E_L\): leak or resting voltage.
  • \(R\): resistance.
  • \(I(t)\): input current.

Meaning of the Equation

The leak makes old input fade instead of accumulating forever. The threshold turns enough recent input into a spike, giving a simple model of neural firing.

Conditions and units

This differential equation describes only subthreshold voltage. A separate threshold, reset voltage, and usually a refractory rule complete the spiking model. In SI, \(V,E_L\) are in volts, \(R\) in ohms, \(I\) in amperes, and \(\tau_m,t\) in seconds, so all terms have voltage units. The usual passive model has \(\tau_m=RC_m>0\), where \(C_m\) is membrane capacitance in farads.

Worked example or interpretation

With illustrative R=100 MΩ and C=100 pF, the membrane time constant is 10 ms. A constant input of 0.1 nA shifts the subthreshold steady voltage by RI=10 mV above rest. Whether a spike occurs depends on the separately specified threshold and reset rule.

Source

Gerstner et al., Neuronal Dynamics, Integrate-and-Fire Models.
