Closed Timelike Curve Condition

Equation

$$ \Delta t_{\rm mouths} > \frac{L}{c} $$

Literal Reading

Delta t subscript mouths is greater than L divided by c.

Common Reading

In an idealized traversable-wormhole construction, the mouth-time offset exceeds the external light-travel time.

Symbols

\(\Delta t_{\rm mouths}\): time offset between the two wormhole mouths.

\(L\): ordinary spatial separation between mouths.

\(c\): speed of light.

Meaning of the Equation

This page uses the \((-+++)\) signature when timelike curves have \(ds^2<0\). With \((+---)\), the timelike sign convention reverses.

Conditions and units

\(\Delta t_{\rm mouths}\) is the positive time advance in the traversal direction being considered (s), measured relative to a common external clock convention. \(L\) is the external separation (m), and \(c\) is light speed (m/s). The estimate assumes nearly stationary mouths, an approximately flat external path, an existing traversable throat and negligible traversal delay. A physical closed timelike route needs the advance to exceed the actual total return and traversal times; \(L/c\) is only the ideal external null-travel threshold. The inequality alone neither constructs such a spacetime nor establishes stability, realizable matter or chronology protection.
