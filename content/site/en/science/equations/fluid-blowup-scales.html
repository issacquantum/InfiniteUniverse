OpenAI Construction: Core Scaling

Equation

$$\begin{gathered}\tau=1-t,\quad 0<h<1/100,\quad\ell_r\asymp\tau^{1/2},\quad\ell_z\asymp\tau^{1/2-h},\\ U_\theta,U_z\asymp\tau^{-1/2-h},\quad U_r=O(\tau^{-1/2}),\\ E_{\mathrm{core}}=O(\tau^{1/2-3h}),\quad\ell_r/\ell_z\asymp\tau^h\end{gathered}$$

Literal Reading

Tau equals one minus t, and h lies strictly between zero and one hundredth. Ell subscript r is comparable to tau to one half; ell subscript z is comparable to tau to one half minus h. U subscript theta and U subscript z are comparable to tau to minus one half minus h; U subscript r is big O of tau to minus one half. E subscript core is big O of tau to one half minus three h, and ell subscript r divided by ell subscript z is comparable to tau to h.

Common Reading

Both core dimensions shrink, speed grows, and the core-energy estimate decreases because the concentrating volume shrinks sufficiently fast.

Symbols

  • \(\tau=1-t\): Time remaining until the normalized singular time.
  • \(h\): Fixed small positive exponent, strictly below \(1/100\).
  • \(\ell_r,\ell_z\): Characteristic radial and axial core lengths.
  • \(U_r,U_\theta,U_z\): Core supremum magnitudes of the leading radial, azimuthal, and axial velocities.
  • \(E_{\mathrm{core}}\): Kinetic energy integrated over the similarity-defined core, not the whole fluid.
  • \(\asymp,O\): Two-sided comparability by positive constants; an upper bound up to a constant, respectively.

Meaning of the Equation

These are asymptotic estimates in Sections 2–3 of the OpenAI paper, not exact profiles. Core volume has order at most \(\ell_r^2\ell_z=\tau^{3/2-h}\); multiplying by \(U_\theta^2+U_z^2\) yields the energy exponent \(1/2-3h>0\). The ratio \(\ell_r/\ell_z\to0\), although both lengths vanish. This core is not a transported material parcel. The full theorem additionally requires all residual cancellations and smooth forcing.
