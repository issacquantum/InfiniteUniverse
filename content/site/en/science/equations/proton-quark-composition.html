Proton Quark Composition

Equation

$$p = uud$$

Literal Reading

p equals uud.

Common Reading

A proton is composed of two up quarks and one down quark.

Symbols

\(p\): proton.

\(u\): up quark.

\(d\): down quark.

Meaning of the Equation

The valence quark count gives the proton its charge and baryon identity, but most of the proton's mass and dynamics come from QCD fields, gluons, and sea quarks.

Conditions and units

This is valence-content notation, not multiplication or a complete three-quark wavefunction. The valence charges sum to \(+2e/3+2e/3-e/3=+e\), where \(e=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\) C exactly. Sea quark-antiquark pairs and gluons do not alter the proton’s total charge.

Worked example or interpretation

Two up-valence charges of +2e/3 and one down-valence charge of −e/3 give total charge +e. The expression labels valence content; summing three bare quark masses would not correctly reproduce the proton mass.

Source
