Wheeler-DeWitt Equation

Equation

$$\hat{H}\Psi = 0$$

Literal Reading

H hat acting on Psi equals zero.

Common Reading

A schematic Hamiltonian constraint in canonical quantum gravity.

Symbols

\(\hat{H}\): Hamiltonian constraint operator.

\(\Psi\): wave functional of geometry and matter fields.

Meaning of the Equation

The equation removes an external time parameter at the total-state level. Ordinary time must then be recovered from internal clocks, correlations, or a semiclassical approximation.

Scope and Limits

This schematic equation suppresses local Hamiltonian constraints, diffeomorphism constraints, factor-ordering choices, regularization, and the physical-inner-product problem.

Conditions and units

The zero is a constraint on allowed states, not a measured zero energy for every local subsystem. The precise dimensions of the constraint operator and wave functional depend on canonical variables, smearing, and normalization, which the schematic display suppresses; assigning one universal numerical value or SI wavefunction unit would be unjustified. The expression belongs to a quantum-gravity framework with unresolved operator and interpretation issues, not an experimentally confirmed replacement for the Schrödinger equation.

Reference

C. Kiefer, The semiclassical approximation to quantum gravity.

Worked example or interpretation

A state can satisfy a total Hamiltonian constraint while correlations between an internal clock and other variables display change. Calling the total constraint “zero” therefore does not say that every internal clock stops or that every subsystem has zero energy. Recovering a viable clock description requires additional approximations or a relational construction.
