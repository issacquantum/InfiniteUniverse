RLHF (reinforcement learning from human feedback) Objective

Equation

$$ \max_\theta\;\mathbb{E}[r_\phi(x,y)]-\beta D_{\rm KL}(\pi_\theta(y|x)\|\pi_{\rm ref}(y|x)) $$

Literal Reading

Maximize over theta the expectation of r subscript phi of x and y, minus beta times the K L divergence between pi subscript theta and the reference pi, both evaluated at y given x.

Common Reading

Maximize reward-model score while penalizing deviation from a reference policy.

Symbols

\(r_\phi(x,y)\): learned reward model score for prompt \(x\) and output \(y\).

\(\pi_\theta\): tuned policy.

\(\pi_{\rm ref}\): reference model.

\(\beta\): strength of the KL penalty.

Meaning of the Equation

The KL term constrains the optimized policy to stay close to the reference policy; reward is learned or estimated, not ground truth.

Conditions and units

This compact form is conditional on a fixed input x: the expectation is over \(y\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid x)\), and the KL divergence compares the full conditional distributions over y. Training over a prompt dataset averages the entire expression over its input distribution. \(\theta\) are policy parameters, \(\phi\) fixed reward-model parameters, and \(\beta\ge0\) converts dimensionless KL into reward units. The penalty encourages proximity but does not impose a hard KL constraint or guarantee truthful outputs.
