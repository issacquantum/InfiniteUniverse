Neutron Quark Composition

Equation

$$n = udd$$

Literal Reading

n equals udd.

Common Reading

A neutron is composed of one up quark and two down quarks.

Symbols

\(n\): neutron.

\(u\): up quark.

\(d\): down quark.

Meaning of the Equation

The notation gives the neutron’s valence-quark content, not a complete three-particle wavefunction. The charges \(+2e/3-e/3-e/3=0\) give its exact net charge. Gluons and sea quark-antiquark pairs also contribute to the neutron’s structure without changing that total. Here \(e=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\) C is the positive elementary charge; the letters label particle types rather than multiplied numerical variables.

Worked example or interpretation

One up-valence charge of +2e/3 and two down-valence charges of −e/3 add to zero. This charge accounting does not imply that the neutron has no internal charged structure or magnetic moment.
