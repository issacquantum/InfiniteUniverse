Equation

$$\|u\|_{W^{k,p}(\Omega)}=\left(\sum_{|\alpha|\leq k}\|D^\alpha u\|_{L^p(\Omega)}^p\right)^{1/p}$$

Literal Reading

The W k p norm is the pth root of the sum of the pth powers of L p norms of derivatives through order k.

Common Reading

Control the function and its weak derivatives together.

Symbols

k: nonnegative integer; 1≤p<∞; α: multi-index; Dᵅ: weak derivative.

Meaning of the Equation

This is an integer-order Sobolev norm. Fractional order and p=∞ require other definitions. Embedding conclusions depend on dimension and domain.
