Geodesic Equation

Equation

$$\frac{d^2x^\mu}{d\tau^2} + \Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}\frac{dx^\alpha}{d\tau}\frac{dx^\beta}{d\tau} = 0$$

Literal Reading

The second derivative of x superscript mu with respect to tau, plus Gamma superscript mu subscript alpha beta times the derivative of x superscript alpha with respect to tau times the derivative of x superscript beta with respect to tau, equals zero.

Common Reading

The equation for free fall motion through curved spacetime.

Symbols

\(x^\mu\): spacetime coordinates along the path.

\(\tau\): proper time along the object's worldline.

\(\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}\): Christoffel symbols, encoding how coordinates curve and change.

\(\alpha,\beta,\mu\): spacetime indices.

Meaning of the Equation

The displayed equation uses proper time \(\tau\) for a freely falling massive test particle. For a null geodesic, proper time cannot parameterize the path; replace \(\tau\) by an affine parameter such as \(\lambda\). Christoffel symbols describe the connection in the chosen coordinates and may be nonzero even in flat spacetime.
