Local Hamiltonian Decision Gap

Equation

$$ H=\sum_{j=1}^{m}H_j, \qquad \lambda_{\min}(H)\le a \quad\text{or}\quad \lambda_{\min}(H)\ge b, \qquad b-a\ge \frac{1}{\mathrm{poly}(n)} $$

Literal Reading

H equals the sum of H subscript j from j equals one to m. The smallest eigenvalue of H is at most a or at least b, with b minus a at least one divided by a polynomial in n.

Common Reading

Decide whether a local quantum system has low energy or is separated from low energy by a promised gap.

Symbols

\(H\): the full Hamiltonian of the system.

\(H_j\): a local term acting on only a few qubits or particles.

\(\lambda_{\min}(H)\): the ground-state energy, the smallest eigenvalue of \(H\).

\(a,b\): threshold energies defining the two promised cases.

Meaning of the Equation

The promise gap \(b-a\ge1/\operatorname{poly}(n)\) is essential to the decision problem.

Conditions and units

Here \(n\) is the number of qubits, \(m\) is a polynomially bounded number of terms, and each Hermitian \(H_j\) acts on a bounded number of qubits with bounded norm and a finite-precision description. Energies are expressed in a fixed normalized unit. The positive polynomial sets an inverse-polynomial resolution, not a physical constant. Inputs with ground energy strictly between \(a\) and \(b\) are outside the promise. This decision gap is not the spectral gap between the lowest two energy levels.
