Qubit Superposition State

Equation

$$ |\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle $$

Literal Reading

Ket psi equals alpha times ket zero plus beta times ket one.

Common Reading

A qubit can combine zero and one with complex amplitudes.

Symbols

\(|\psi\rangle\): the full quantum state of the qubit.

\(\alpha\): the complex amplitude attached to the basis state \(|0\rangle\).

\(\beta\): the complex amplitude attached to the basis state \(|1\rangle\).

\(|0\rangle\): the computational basis state zero.

\(|1\rangle\): the computational basis state one.

Meaning of the Equation

Amplitudes are complex numbers whose relative phases affect interference. For a normalized state, their squared magnitudes give the probabilities of the corresponding computational-basis outcomes before measurement; a different measurement basis requires the amplitudes in that basis.

Conditions and units

The basis kets are orthonormal and the dimensionless amplitudes obey \(|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1\). A common global phase does not change the physical state. This is a pure qubit, not the most general mixed state, and the labels zero and one are basis labels rather than energies or simultaneous classical outcomes.
