Decohered Record State

Equation

$$ \rho_X \approx \sum_\alpha p_\alpha |\alpha\rangle\langle\alpha| $$

Literal Reading

rho sub X is approximately equal to the sum over alpha of p alpha times ket alpha bra alpha.

Common Reading

After decoherence, the local state behaves like a weighted mixture of stable record alternatives.

Symbols

\(\rho_X\): the reduced state of the local record-bearing subsystem.

\(\approx\): approximately equal, because small interference terms are ignored at the effective branch level.

\(\sum_\alpha\): the sum over the stable record alternatives.

\(p_\alpha\): Born-rule weight / effective classical probability weight for record alternative \(\alpha\), after decoherence in the chosen pointer basis.

\(|\alpha\rangle\langle\alpha|\): the projector onto one stable record state.

Meaning of the Equation

The equation describes the effective classical-looking form of a local state after its environment has suppressed usable interference between different record alternatives. It is not a collapse law. A branch-relative account of an observer is an interpretation of this effective description; the reduced-state equation alone does not select one experienced outcome.

Conditions and units

\(X\) labels the subsystem, \(\alpha\) an approximately orthogonal pointer basis, and \(p_\alpha\ge0\) are dimensionless weights summing to one. Density operators and projectors are dimensionless. The approximation depends on the interaction, timescale and observables accessible to the subsystem; it is not an exact assertion that all off-diagonal terms vanish.
