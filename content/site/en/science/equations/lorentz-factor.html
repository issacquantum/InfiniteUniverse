Lorentz Factor

Equation

$$\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}$$

Literal Reading

gamma equals 1 over square root of 1 minus v to the power of 2 over c to the power of 2.

Common Reading

The scale factor that controls relativistic time dilation, length contraction, and energy growth.

Symbols

\(\gamma\): Lorentz factor.

\(v\): relative speed between inertial frames.

\(c\): speed of light in vacuum.

Meaning of the Equation

Defined for \(|v|<c\). As \(v\) approaches \(c\), \(\gamma\) grows without bound.

Conditions and units

Both \(v\) and \(c=299792458\) m/s use the same speed units, so \(\gamma\) is dimensionless and at least one. This factor refers to subluminal inertial relative motion in special relativity; it does not define a rest frame for a photon.

Worked example or interpretation

At v=0.6c, the expression gives 1/√0.64=1.25. At zero speed it is one. The denominator tends to zero as the magnitude of v approaches c from below; this limiting statement does not assign a finite Lorentz factor to light.

Source

Sean M. Carroll, Lecture Notes on General Relativity.
