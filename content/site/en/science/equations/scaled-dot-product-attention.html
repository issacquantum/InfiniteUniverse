Scaled Dot Product Attention

Equation

$$ \operatorname{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\operatorname{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V $$

Literal Reading

Attention of Q, K, and V equals softmax of Q times K transpose divided by the square root of d subscript k, with the resulting matrix multiplied by V.

Common Reading

Attention compares queries and keys, applies softmax normalization, and uses the resulting weights to combine value vectors.

Symbols

\(Q\): query matrix.

\(K\): key matrix.

\(V\): value matrix.

\(d_k\): key dimension, used to stabilize the scale of dot products.

\(\operatorname{softmax}\): converts scores into weights that sum to one.

Meaning of the Equation

Attention scores decide how strongly each token uses information from other tokens. The scaling keeps dot products numerically controlled before the softmax turns them into weights.

Conditions and units

For n queries and m keys, Q has shape \(n\times d_k\), K \(m\times d_k\), and V \(m\times d_v\); the result is \(n\times d_v\). T means transpose. Softmax acts across each row of key scores, yielding nonnegative weights that sum to one. Scores are dimensionless. The displayed form has no causal or padding mask; those restrictions require masking before normalization. The scale reduces variance growth under standard component assumptions but is not an absolute bound on score magnitude.

Worked example or interpretation

For one query with two equal key scores, rowwise softmax assigns weights 1/2 and 1/2. If the corresponding scalar values are 2 and 6, the output is 4. Equal scores lead to equal weights regardless of their common finite offset.

Notation details

Softmax exponentiates each score and divides it by the sum of exponentials in the same row. The key dimension d_k is a positive integer, and the square root divides every entry of the score matrix before this normalization. V is multiplied afterward.

Source

Vaswani et al., Attention Is All You Need.
