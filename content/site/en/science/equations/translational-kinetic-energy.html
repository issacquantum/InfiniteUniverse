Translational Kinetic Energy

Equation

$$K=\tfrac12mv^2$$

Literal reading

K equals one half times m times v squared.

Conceptual reading

Translational kinetic energy grows linearly with mass and quadratically with speed.

Symbols

\(K\) is kinetic energy, \(m\) mass and \(v\) speed, the magnitude of velocity. Exponent 2 squares the speed; one half is a dimensionless factor.

Values, units and domains

Mass is in kg, speed in m/s and energy in J = kg·m²/s². Mass and speed are nonnegative; energy depends on the reference frame.

Meaning

Doubling speed quadruples kinetic energy for the same mass. Direction does not enter because this energy is scalar.

Assumptions and limits

Newtonian translational energy at speeds small compared with light speed. Extended bodies can also have rotational and internal energy.

Example

A 2 kg body at 3 m/s has kinetic energy 0.5 × 2 × 9 = 9 J.
