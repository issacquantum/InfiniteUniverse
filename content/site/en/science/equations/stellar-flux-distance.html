Luminosity and Observed Flux

Equation

$$F=\frac{L}{4\pi d^2}$$

Literal reading

Observed flux equals luminosity divided by the area of a sphere at the observer’s distance.

Symbols and units

F: bolometric energy flux, W/m²; L: bolometric luminosity, W; d: distance, m; pi: dimensionless.

Physical meaning

Geometric spreading changes apparent brightness without changing intrinsic luminosity.

Derivation

Conserve the emitted power across concentric spheres and divide it by spherical area.

Assumptions and limits

Isotropic emission, negligible extinction, ordinary nearby-source geometry. Cosmological sources require luminosity distance; band-limited measurements need spectral corrections.

Worked example

The same star at twice the distance supplies one quarter of the flux.
