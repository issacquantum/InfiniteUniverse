Introduction

The Big Bang theory, our leading cosmological model, is a testament to cumulative human endeavor, built upon the insights and discoveries of numerous brilliant scientists. This section honors the key figures whose theoretical predictions, observational breakthroughs, and meticulous research shaped our understanding of the universe's origin and evolution.

The modern version of this story continues through large collaborations and instruments as much as through individual genius. Planck refined the cosmic microwave background parameters, JWST is testing early galaxy formation, DESI and Euclid are mapping large-scale structure and dark energy, and gravitational-wave observatories are adding an independent way to study compact objects and cosmic history. This section keeps the historical figures, but the living frontier is collaborative, data-heavy, and constantly checked against new observations.
