Kohn-Sham Equations

Kohn-Sham Equations

Equation

$$\left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\nabla^2+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(\mathbf{r})\right]\psi_i=\epsilon_i\psi_i$$

Literal Reading

open bracket minus h-bar squared over two m times nabla squared plus V effective of r close bracket psi sub i equals epsilon sub i psi sub i.

Common Reading

A hard many-electron problem is replaced by easier one-electron-like equations with an effective potential.

Symbols

  • \(\psi_i\): Kohn-Sham orbital.
  • \(V_{\mathrm{eff}}\): effective potential.
  • \(\epsilon_i\): orbital energy parameter.
  • \(\nabla^2\): Laplacian / spatial second-derivative operator.

Meaning of the Equation

Kohn-Sham orbitals are auxiliary orbitals used to reproduce the electron density; they are not generally the true many-body electron wavefunction.
