Dimensionless Spin Parameter

Dimensionless Spin Parameter

Equation

$$ a_* = \frac{cJ}{GM^2} $$

Literal Reading

a star equals c J divided by G M squared.

Common Reading

The normalized spin of a Kerr black hole.

Symbols

\(a_*\): dimensionless spin parameter.

\(J\): angular momentum.

\(M\): black hole mass.

\(G\): gravitational constant.

\(c\): speed of light.

Meaning of the Equation

For Kerr black holes, \(0\le |a_*|\le1\) in classical general relativity. Values outside that range would not describe an ordinary Kerr black-hole horizon.

Scope and Limits

The parameter is defined for Kerr-like compact objects. Measurements of \(a_*\) depend on model assumptions about disks, spectra, or gravitational waves, so the number is physical but not directly visible by itself.

Conditions and units

\(J\) is angular momentum in kg m²/s, \(M\) mass in kg, \(G\) in m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻² and \(c\) in m/s. These units cancel. The notation \(\chi\) in the article denotes the same parameter; it differs from the Kerr length \(a=J/(Mc)\). The horizon bound stated here applies to uncharged Kerr solutions.

Equivalent notation

$$\chi=\frac{cJ}{GM^2}$$The article also denotes the same dimensionless spin by \(\chi\), read chi equals c J divided by G M squared.

Worked example or interpretation

If angular momentum is J=GM²/(2c), the dimensionless spin is 0.5. Reversing the spin axis reverses its sign while leaving its magnitude unchanged. The Kerr length a then equals half of GM/c².

Source

Sean M. Carroll, Lecture Notes on General Relativity.

Physical constants

G≈6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ m³ kg⁻¹ s⁻²; c=299792458 m/s exactly. NIST, CODATA 2022.
