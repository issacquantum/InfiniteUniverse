Construction: Radial Speed Bound

Construction: Radial Speed Bound

Equation

$$U_r=O(\tau^{-1/2})$$

Literal reading

U subscript r is big O of tau raised to minus one half.

Conceptual reading

The radial speed has an upper growth bound of inverse square-root order.

Symbols

\(U_r\) is the nonnegative characteristic magnitude of leading radial velocity, and \(\tau\) remaining normalized time. \(O\) means bounded above by a constant multiple for sufficiently small positive tau, not equality or a matching lower bound.

Values, units and domains

These are normalized construction variables with singular time 1. Here \(\tau=1-t>0\) is remaining time and \(0<h<1/100\) a fixed dimensionless exponent. Physical lengths, times and velocities require reference scales; a power of normalized time is not an SI dimensional equality.

Meaning

The bound distinguishes radial motion from the stronger characteristic azimuthal and axial scaling. By itself it does not assert that radial speed diverges.

Assumptions and limits

This summarizes a leading-core estimate in the cited forced construction, not a universal flow law or an independent verification of the proof. The full result also requires the residual cancellations and smooth forcing. The similarity-defined core is not a transported material parcel.

Example

For 0<tau≤1, a bounded radial speed of 1 satisfies an inverse-square-root upper bound. This simple example shows why big O alone cannot establish blowup.
