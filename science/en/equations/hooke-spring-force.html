Hooke’s Spring Force

Hooke’s Spring Force

Equation

$$F_x=-kx$$

Literal reading

F subscript x equals minus k times x.

Conceptual reading

A linear spring exerts a restoring force opposite displacement from equilibrium.

Symbols

\(F_x\) is the x component of force, \(x\) displacement from equilibrium and \(k\) the positive spring stiffness. The minus sign indicates opposition to displacement; the subscript specifies the axis.

Values, units and domains

Force is in N, displacement in m and stiffness in N/m. k depends on the spring; it is not a universal constant.

Meaning

Positive displacement gives negative force, and negative displacement gives positive force. Equilibrium has zero spring force in this coordinate convention.

Assumptions and limits

This is a linear elastic approximation. Large deformation, yielding, hysteresis or damping need additional terms or another model.

Example

With k = 100 N/m and x = 0.02 m, the spring force is −2 N.

Source

MIT OpenCourseWare, 8.01SC Classical Mechanics (2016).
