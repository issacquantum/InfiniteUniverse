Neural Scaling Law

Neural Scaling Law

Equation

$$ L(N,D)\approx L_\infty+aN^{-\alpha}+bD^{-\beta} $$

Literal Reading

L of N comma D is approximately L infinity plus a N to the minus alpha plus b D to the minus beta.

Common Reading

For many language-model regimes, performance changes predictably as parameters, data, and compute scale.

Symbols

\(N\): model parameter count.

\(D\): dataset size.

\(L_\infty\): irreducible loss floor.

\(a,b\): empirical coefficients that set the size of each scaling term.

\(\alpha,\beta\): empirical scaling exponents.

Meaning of the Equation

This is an empirical scaling ansatz, not a fundamental law; constants depend on architecture, data, compute, and training regime.
