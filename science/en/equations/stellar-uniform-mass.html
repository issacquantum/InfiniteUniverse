Mass in a Uniform-Density Sphere

Mass in a Uniform-Density Sphere

Equation

$$M(r)=\frac{4\pi}{3}\rho_0 r^3$$

Literal reading

Enclosed mass equals four pi over three times the constant density times radius cubed.

Symbols and units

M(r): enclosed mass in kg; r: distance from the center in m; rho₀: constant mass density in kg/m³; pi: dimensionless circle constant. R, used below, is the outer radius in m.

Physical meaning

At constant density, mass grows as enclosed volume. Half the radius contains one eighth of the total mass, not half.

Derivation

Start with the mass-continuity equation. Replace density by the constant rho₀ and integrate from the center to r. The integral of radius squared is radius cubed divided by three. The central condition M(0)=0 removes the integration constant.

Assumptions and limits

A spherical continuum with uniform density and no central point mass, from the center to outer radius R. This is not a realistic stellar density profile.

Worked example

With rho₀=1,000 kg/m³ and R=1.0 × 10⁹ m, total mass is about 4.189 × 10³⁰ kg. At r=R/2 it is 5.236 × 10²⁹ kg. The next calculation uses that interior mass, not the full mass, for local gravity.
