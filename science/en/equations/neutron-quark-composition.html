Neutron Quark Composition

Neutron Quark Composition

Equation

$$n = udd$$

Literal Reading

n equals udd.

Common Reading

A neutron is composed of one up quark and two down quarks.

Symbols

\(n\): neutron.

\(u\): up quark.

\(d\): down quark.

Meaning of the Equation

A neutron contains one up valence quark and two down valence quarks, giving it zero net electric charge before including the full sea of QCD excitations.
