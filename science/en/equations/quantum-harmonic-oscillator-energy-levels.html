Quantum Harmonic Oscillator Energy Levels

Quantum Harmonic Oscillator Energy Levels

Equation

$$ E_n = \hbar\omega\left(n + \frac{1}{2}\right), \quad n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots $$

Literal Reading

E sub n equals h minus bar ω the quantity n plus 1 over 2.

Common Reading

The oscillator has equally spaced quantum energies with a nonzero zero-point ground state.

Symbols

\(E_n\): the allowed energy of level \(n\).

\(\hbar\): the reduced Planck constant.

\(\omega\): the angular frequency of the oscillator.

\(n\): the excitation level, starting at zero.

\(1/2\): the zero-point contribution that never disappears.

Meaning of the Equation

The zero-point term means the oscillator cannot have zero energy. The evenly spaced ladder underlies photons, phonons, and many small-oscillation models.
