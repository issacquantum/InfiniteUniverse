Entropy Change of an Isolated Total System

Entropy Change of an Isolated Total System

Equation

$$\Delta S_{\rm universe}\ge0$$

Literal reading

Delta S subscript universe is greater than or equal to zero.

Conceptual reading

The combined entropy change of a system and all relevant surroundings cannot be negative in macroscopic thermodynamics.

Symbols

\(S_{\rm universe}\) is the total thermodynamic entropy of the system plus surroundings; the subscript here labels that isolated composite, not necessarily the observable cosmos. \(\Delta\) means final minus initial and \(\ge\) means greater than or equal to.

Values, units and domains

Entropy change is in J/K. The bound zero has the same units implicitly. The total must include reservoirs and other parts receiving heat or entropy.

Meaning

Local ordering or cooling is consistent with the law if entropy rises elsewhere by at least as much. Equality describes the reversible limit.

Assumptions and limits

Macroscopic second-law statement for an isolated composite with well-defined thermodynamic entropy. It is not a claim that every microscopic fluctuation increases entropy or that arbitrary quantum subsystem entropy is monotonic.

Example

Transferring 600 J from a 400 K reservoir to a 300 K reservoir changes their total entropy by −600/400 + 600/300 = 0.5 J/K.

Source

David Tong, Statistical Physics; NIST, CODATA 2022.
