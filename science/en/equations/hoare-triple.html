Hoare Triple

Hoare Triple

Equation

$$ \{P\}\;C\;\{Q\} $$

Literal Reading

open brace P close brace C open brace Q close brace.

Common Reading

If the precondition holds and the command terminates, the postcondition holds.

Symbols

\(P\): precondition, what must be true before execution.

\(C\): command or program fragment.

\(Q\): postcondition, what must be true afterward.

Meaning of the Equation

Under the usual partial-correctness interpretation, the triple asserts that execution from a state satisfying P, if it terminates, yields a state satisfying Q. The assertion needs a proof; writing it does not prove it. Total correctness additionally requires termination.
