System State Conditioned on a Clock Reading

System State Conditioned on a Clock Reading

Equation

$$|\psi(t)\rangle\propto\langle t|\Psi\rangle$$

Literal reading

Ket psi of t is proportional to bra t contracted with ket capital psi.

Conceptual reading

Conditioning the joint state on a clock reading leaves a state of the remaining system.

Symbols

\(|\Psi\rangle\) is the joint clock-system ket; \(\langle t|\) is the adjoint clock state labelled by reading \(t\). The product is a partial inner product over the clock alone, leaving a system ket \(|\psi(t)\rangle\). \(\propto\) allows normalization by a nonzero factor; it is not equality of already normalized vectors.

Values, units and domains

The clock label is in s when calibrated as time. Finite-dimensional normalized-state amplitudes are dimensionless. Ideal continuous clock kets can be generalized and carry normalization conventions; their conditional state is normalized only when the contraction is nonzero.

Meaning

Correlation lets different clock readings select different conditional system states even if the joint state is stationary. The construction is relational rather than an added external time parameter.

Assumptions and limits

A specified clock-system factorization and clock states are needed. Schrödinger evolution additionally requires suitable clock covariance and Hamiltonian assumptions. Real clock interactions and limited resolution can alter the effective dynamics.

Example

In a two-reading toy state (|t₀⟩|0⟩ + |t₁⟩|1⟩)/√2 with orthogonal clock states, conditioning on t₀ gives |0⟩ after normalization, while t₁ gives |1⟩. This illustrates conditioning, not a continuous ideal clock.

Source

Don N. Page and William K. Wootters, Evolution without evolution: Dynamics described by stationary observables (1983); Moreva et al., Time from quantum entanglement: an experimental illustration.
