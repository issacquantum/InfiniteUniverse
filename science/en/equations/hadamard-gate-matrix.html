Hadamard Gate Matrix

Hadamard Gate Matrix

Equation

$$ H = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix} $$

Literal Reading

H equals of 1 over square root of 2 open bracket open bracket 1 comma 1 close bracket comma open bracket 1 comma minus 1 close bracket close bracket.

Common Reading

The Hadamard gate turns basis states into balanced superpositions.

Symbols

\(H\): the Hadamard gate.

\(\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\): the normalization factor that keeps the operation unitary.

\(\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{pmatrix}\): the matrix form of the transformation in the \(|0\rangle, |1\rangle\) basis.

Meaning of the Equation

Hadamard sends basis states into balanced superpositions with signs that matter for interference. It is useful because later gates can turn those signs into amplified or cancelled measurement outcomes.
