Newton's Second Law

Newton's Second Law

Equation

$$\mathbf{F}=m\mathbf{a}$$

Literal Reading

F equals m times a.

Common Reading

The net force acting on an object equals its mass times its acceleration.

Symbols

  • \(\mathbf{F}\): total force on the object.
  • \(m\): mass.
  • \(\mathbf{a}\): acceleration.

Meaning of the Equation

This form assumes constant mass and an inertial reference frame in the Newtonian regime. The force is the net external force. Variable-mass systems require momentum balance including mass flow; relativistic dynamics uses relativistic momentum.
