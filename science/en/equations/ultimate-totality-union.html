Ultimate Totality as a Union of Modes

Ultimate Totality as a Union of Modes

Equation

$$\Omega=\bigcup_{m\in M}\Omega_m$$

Literal reading

Capital omega equals the union, over m belonging to capital M, of capital omega subscript m. The subscript labels a mode; it is not multiplication.

Conceptual reading

This personal definition collects forms that belong to at least one mode of existence. It is an ontological proposal, not a physical law.

Symbols

\(\Omega\) is the total domain of forms in this framework; \(M\) is the collection of modes; \(m\) labels one mode; \(\Omega_m\) is its domain. The symbol \(\in\) means membership. The indexed union \(\bigcup\) collects the members of the indicated domains. The equals sign defines the collection.

Values, units and domains

These are domains and labels with no SI units or numerical constants. Omega is neither a solid angle nor a cosmological density parameter. The letter m does not denote mass or metres. Domains may overlap.

Meaning

A form is included if it belongs to a mode in M. The union does not retain a separate copy for each mode. Mathematical, conceptual, possible, counterfactual and counterpossible content need not be physically instantiated.

Assumptions and limits

This is schematic set notation within my framework, not a completed axiomatization of a universal set or a proof of mathematical consistency. It establishes neither physical existence nor a mechanism connecting physical organization and first-person experience.

Example

A description of a triangle can belong to a mathematical domain and a conceptual domain. The union includes the form once; it does not imply that a corresponding material triangle exists.

Source

Issac Tabares, My Theory.
