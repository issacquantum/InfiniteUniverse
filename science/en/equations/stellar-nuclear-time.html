Nuclear Fuel Timescale

Nuclear Fuel Timescale

Equation

$$t_{\rm nuc}\sim\frac{f\eta Mc^2}{L}$$

Literal reading

The fuel time is of order usable fuel fraction times conversion efficiency times rest energy, divided by luminosity.

Symbols and units

tnuc: s; f: dimensionless usable fuel mass divided by total mass; eta: dimensionless energy-conversion efficiency; M: kg; c: m/s; L: representative luminosity, W.

Physical meaning

Fuel access and consumption rate both matter; more total mass does not guarantee longer life.

Derivation

Multiply usable fuel mass by the energy released per unit fuel mass and divide by an approximately representative luminosity.

Assumptions and limits

A reservoir estimate, not a universal evolution law. Composition, mixing, neutrino losses, and varying luminosity change f and effective eta.

Worked example

Illustrative solar values f=0.1×0.7 and eta=0.007 give about 7.3 billion years at present luminosity, of order ten billion years. Evolution calculations do not hold that luminosity fixed.

Source

Princeton: Equations of Stellar Structure
