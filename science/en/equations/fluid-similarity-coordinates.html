OpenAI Construction: Similarity Coordinates

Equation

$$\tau=q(1-\eta^2),\qquad z=q^D\eta,\qquad X=\frac{r^2}{2q},\qquad q>0,\quad |\eta|<1$$

Literal Reading

Tau equals q times the quantity one minus eta squared. z equals q to the power D times eta. X equals r squared divided by two q, with q positive and the absolute value of eta less than one.

Common Reading

The coordinates separate the radial and axial scales of the concentrating core.

Symbols

  • \(\tau=1-t\): Time remaining before the normalized singular time.
  • \(r,z\): Cylindrical radius and axial position.
  • \(D=\frac12-h,\quad 0<h<1/100\): Axial scaling exponent and fixed small parameter.
  • \(q=q(z,\tau)>0\): Auxiliary scale, not an integrability exponent.
  • \(X,\eta\): Rescaled squared radius and axial coordinate.

Meaning of the Equation

From the displayed relations, q equals tau divided by one minus eta squared. Away from eta equal to plus or minus one, q is comparable to tau. Bounded X therefore corresponds to a radial scale proportional to the square root of q, while bounded eta gives an axial scale q to the power D. The core is defined by these coordinates, not by a fixed collection of fluid particles.

Scope and Limits

These are coordinates for the reported construction before the singular time. They are not the isotropic scaling symmetry of general Navier–Stokes solutions and do not establish blowup on their own.
