Measurement Effect Operator

Measurement Effect Operator

Equation

$$E_m=M_m^\dagger M_m$$

Literal reading

E subscript m equals M subscript m dagger times M subscript m.

Conceptual reading

A measurement operator determines a positive effect that gives the probability of its outcome.

Symbols

\(m\) labels a measurement outcome; \(M_m\) is its measurement operator; dagger denotes the Hermitian adjoint. \(E_m\) is the effect operator. Juxtaposition is ordered operator multiplication, applied right to left.

Values, units and domains

In finite-dimensional normalized bases these operators are dimensionless. For a complete measurement, each effect is positive and bounded above by the identity. M can be non-Hermitian.

Meaning

The effect specifies outcome statistics but not the entire post-measurement change. Different measurement operators may have the same effect.

Assumptions and limits

This expression uses one measurement operator per recorded outcome. An outcome collecting several Kraus operators has an effect equal to the sum of their adjoint-products.

Example

For the projector onto qubit state |0⟩, the adjoint is the same projector and squaring it leaves it unchanged. Its effect is therefore that same projector.

Source

John Watrous, The Theory of Quantum Information (2018).
