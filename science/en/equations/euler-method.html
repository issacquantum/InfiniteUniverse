Euler Method

Euler Method

Equation

$$ y_{n+1}=y_n+h f(t_n,y_n) $$

Literal Reading

y sub n plus 1 equals y sub n plus h f of t sub n comma y sub n.

Common Reading

This advances a system forward using a local derivative as an approximation to the next state.

Symbols

\(y_n\): current state.

\(y_{n+1}\): next state.

\(h\): step size.

\(f(t_n,y_n)\): derivative or slope at the current point.

Meaning of the Equation

Euler assumes the derivative stays nearly constant during one small step. Accuracy depends on making the step short enough that the true curve does not bend too much.
