Bell State Phi Plus

Bell State Phi Plus

Equation

$$ |\Phi^{+}\rangle = \frac{|00\rangle + |11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}} $$

Literal Reading

Ket phi plus equals the sum of ket zero-zero and ket one-one, divided by the square root of two.

Common Reading

One of the maximally entangled Bell states, where two qubits are equally shared between the outcomes zero zero and one one.

Symbols

\(|\Phi^{+}\rangle\): the specific Bell state being named.

\(|00\rangle\): both qubits in state zero.

\(|11\rangle\): both qubits in state one.

\(\sqrt{2}\): the normalization factor that keeps the total probability equal to one.

\(+\): the two basis states add coherently with the same relative phase.

Meaning of the Equation

Measurements of both qubits in the computational (Z) basis give 00 or 11 with equal probability. Matching X measurements also agree, whereas matching Y measurements are anticorrelated. Agreement in every matching basis is not a property of this Bell state. Each individual qubit has a maximally mixed reduced state.
