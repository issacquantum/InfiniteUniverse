Dijkstra Relaxation

Dijkstra Relaxation

Equation

$$ \text{if }d[v]>d[u]+w(u,v),\quad d[v]\leftarrow d[u]+w(u,v) $$

Literal Reading

if d of v is greater than d of u plus w of u comma v, then d of v is updated to d of u plus w of u comma v.

Common Reading

If passing through \(u\) gives a shorter route to \(v\), the algorithm updates the best known distance.

Symbols

\(d[v]\): best known distance to node \(v\).

\(d[u]\): best known distance to node \(u\).

\(w(u,v)\): weight of the edge from \(u\) to \(v\).

Meaning of the Equation

Dijkstra’s algorithm assumes nonnegative edge weights.
