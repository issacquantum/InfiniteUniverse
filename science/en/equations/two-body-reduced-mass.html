Reduced Mass

Reduced Mass

Equation

$$\mu=\frac{Mm}{M+m}$$

Literal reading

Mu equals the product M times m divided by the sum M plus m.

Conceptual reading

Reduced mass is the effective inertia of the relative coordinate in a two-body system.

Symbols

\(\mu\) is reduced mass; \(M,m\) are the two positive masses. Juxtaposition forms their product; the denominator sums them. All three symbols denote scalar masses.

Values, units and domains

All masses are in kg. The quotient has units kg, is positive and smaller than either input mass. There is no universal numerical value.

Meaning

Translation of the centre of mass separates from relative motion, whose kinetic-energy coefficient is the reduced mass. When one mass is much smaller, the reduced mass approaches that smaller mass.

Assumptions and limits

Newtonian two-body change of coordinates with positive masses. Reduced mass is not the sum of masses or an extra constituent.

Example

For M = 3 kg and m = 1 kg, μ = 3/4 kg = 0.75 kg.

Source

MIT OpenCourseWare, 8.01SC Classical Mechanics (2016).
