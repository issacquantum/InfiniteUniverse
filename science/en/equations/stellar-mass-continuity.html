Enclosed Stellar Mass

Enclosed Stellar Mass

Equation

$$\frac{dM}{dr}=4\pi r^2\rho$$

Literal reading

The radial derivative of enclosed mass equals four pi times radius squared times density.

Symbols and units

M(r): enclosed mass, kg; r: radius, m; rho: local mass density, kg/m³; pi: dimensionless circle constant. The derivative has units kg/m.

Physical meaning

Positive density makes enclosed mass increase outward. This mass supplies the gravity in hydrostatic balance.

Derivation

A thin spherical shell has area 4 pi r² and thickness dr. Multiply its volume by density, divide the added mass by dr, and take the limit.

Assumptions and limits

Spherical symmetry and a continuum density profile. Use a regular center with M(0)=0. It is geometric mass bookkeeping, not an energy equation.

Worked example

For uniform density, integration gives enclosed mass proportional to radius cubed; doubling radius encloses eight times as much mass. Real stellar density is not uniform.

Source

Princeton: Equations of Stellar Structure
