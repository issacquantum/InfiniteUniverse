Boltzmann Entropy

Boltzmann Entropy

Equation

$$S_B=k_B\log W$$

Literal Reading

S subscript B equals k subscript B times the logarithm of W.

Common Reading

Boltzmann entropy is proportional to the logarithm of the number of microstates compatible with the macrostate.

Symbols

  • \(S_B\): Boltzmann entropy.
  • \(k_B\): Boltzmann constant.
  • \(W\): number of compatible microstates.

Meaning of the Equation

It is not just a vague word for disorder; it counts how many microscopic arrangements realize the same macroscopic description.
