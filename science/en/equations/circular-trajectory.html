Uniform Circular Trajectory

Uniform Circular Trajectory

Equation

$$\mathbf r(t)=R(\cos\omega t,0,\sin\omega t)$$

Literal reading

Vector r of t equals R times the ordered coordinate triple cosine omega t, zero, sine omega t.

Conceptual reading

The position traces a circle in the x-z plane.

Symbols

\(\mathbf r(t)\) is position relative to the circle centre; \(R\) is radius, \(\omega\) constant angular rate and \(t\) time. Parentheses give the x, y and z components. Sine and cosine act on the phase omega times t; the middle component is zero.

Values, units and domains

R is positive and measured in m, time in s and angular rate in rad/s. The trigonometric factors are dimensionless; position is in m.

Meaning

The radius remains R while the phase advances uniformly. Zero phase places the particle on the positive x axis.

Assumptions and limits

Prescribed planar circular motion with constant radius and angular rate. This parametrization does not identify the force that causes it.

Example

At t = 0 and R = 2 m the position is (2, 0, 0) m. After a quarter-turn it is (0, 0, 2) m for positive angular rate.

Source

MIT OpenCourseWare, 8.01SC Classical Mechanics (2016).
