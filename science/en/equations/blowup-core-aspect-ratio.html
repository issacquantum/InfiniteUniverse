Construction: Core Aspect Ratio

Construction: Core Aspect Ratio

Equation

$$\ell_r/\ell_z\asymp\tau^h$$

Literal reading

Ell subscript r divided by ell subscript z is comparable to tau to the power h.

Conceptual reading

The core becomes relatively more slender as it contracts.

Symbols

\(\ell_r\) and \(\ell_z\) are positive characteristic radial and axial lengths. Their quotient is the dimensionless aspect ratio. \(\tau\) is remaining normalized time, \(h\) a fixed positive exponent, and \(\asymp\) two-sided comparability by positive constants.

Values, units and domains

These are normalized construction variables with singular time 1. Here \(\tau=1-t>0\) is remaining time and \(0<h<1/100\) a fixed dimensionless exponent. Physical lengths, times and velocities require reference scales; a power of normalized time is not an SI dimensional equality.

Meaning

Dividing the radial and axial scales subtracts their exponents: one half minus (one half minus h) equals h. The ratio tends to zero although both lengths tend to zero.

Assumptions and limits

This summarizes a leading-core estimate in the cited forced construction, not a universal flow law or an independent verification of the proof. The full result also requires the residual cancellations and smooth forcing. The similarity-defined core is not a transported material parcel.

Example

For h=0.005, tau^h approaches zero very slowly. Small positive exponents still imply a vanishing limit; a drawing at one finite time need not resemble the eventual limiting aspect ratio.
