Black Hole First Law

Black Hole First Law

Equation

$$dM = \frac{\kappa}{8\pi G}dA + \Omega_H dJ + \Phi_H dQ$$

Literal Reading

d M equals kappa over eight pi G times d A, plus omega subscript H times d J, plus phi subscript H times d Q.

Common Reading

Changes in black-hole mass are related to horizon area, angular momentum, and charge.

Symbols

\(dM\): change in black hole mass.

\(\kappa\): surface gravity.

\(dA\): change in horizon area.

\(\Omega_H\): angular velocity of the horizon.

\(dJ\): change in angular momentum.

\(\Phi_H dQ\): electric potential term.

Meaning of the Equation

This form uses units with \(c=1\), so mass and energy have the same units. The area term corresponds to temperature times the entropy change, while rotation and charge contribute work terms. It relates nearby stationary black-hole solutions.

Scope and Limits

This is a relation between nearby stationary black-hole states. It is not a full time-evolution equation for violent mergers, turbulent accretion, or the microscopic origin of entropy.
