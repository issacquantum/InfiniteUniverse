OpenAI Construction: Support, Energy, and Blowup

Equation

$$\begin{gathered}\operatorname{supp}\mathbf u(\cdot,t)\cup\operatorname{supp}p(\cdot,t)\subset K\quad(0\leq t<1),\\ \sup_{0\leq t<1}\|\mathbf u(t)\|_{L^2(\mathbb R^3)}<\infty,\qquad\limsup_{t\uparrow1}\|\mathbf u(t)\|_{L^\infty(\mathbb R^3)}=\infty.\end{gathered}$$

Literal Reading

The union of the supports of u and p at time t is contained in K for zero less than or equal to t less than one. The supremum, over those times, of the L two norm of u is finite, while the limit superior of its L infinity norm as t approaches one from below is infinite.

Common Reading

The stated construction combines fixed spatial support and uniformly bounded energy with unbounded peak speed.

Symbols

  • \(\operatorname{supp}\): Spatial support: the closure of the set where a field is nonzero.
  • \(K\): A fixed compact subset of three-dimensional space.
  • \(\mathbf u,p\): Velocity and pressure divided by constant density.
  • \(\sup,\limsup\): Supremum over the interval and limiting supremum approaching the endpoint.
  • \(L^2,L^\infty\): Spatial square-integral norm and essential-supremum norm.
  • \(t\uparrow1\): Approach to the normalized singular time from below.

Meaning of the Equation

The square-integral bound controls total kinetic energy, not the largest speed. A field may concentrate on a shrinking region without violating that integral bound. These conditions describe the claimed result, not a proof that any such concentration solves momentum and incompressibility.

Scope and Limits

The statement concerns the forced construction reported by OpenAI: positive viscosity, zero initial velocity, and a smooth force compactly supported in space and time. It is not a claim about every flow or the unforced problem.
