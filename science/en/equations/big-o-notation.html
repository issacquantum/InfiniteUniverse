Big O Notation

Big O Notation

Equation

$$ f(n)=O(g(n)) $$

Literal Reading

f of n equals O of g of n.

Common Reading

The growth of \(f(n)\) is bounded above by a constant multiple of \(g(n)\) for sufficiently large inputs.

Symbols

\(f(n)\): resource use being analyzed, such as time or memory.

\(g(n)\): comparison growth function.

\(n\): input size.

Meaning of the Equation

Big-O is an asymptotic upper bound, not an exact runtime.
