Expectation and variance

Equation

$$\mu=\mathbb E[X]=\int_\Omega X\,dP,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=\mathbb E[(X-\mu)^2]$$

Literal Reading

Mu equals the expectation of X, the integral of X with respect to P; variance is the expectation of the squared centered variable.

Common Reading

Mean and mean squared deviation.

Symbols

X: random variable; μ: mean; P: probability measure.

Meaning of the Equation

A finite second moment guarantees a finite mean and variance. For discrete laws the expectation is a weighted sum; for densities it is an integral against the density.
