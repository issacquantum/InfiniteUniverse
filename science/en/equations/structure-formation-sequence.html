Structure Formation

Equation

$$\text{molecules} \rightarrow \text{stars, planets, and matter structures}$$

Literal Reading

Molecules lead to stars, planets, and structures of matter in this schematic sequence.

Common Reading

Molecular matter eventually participates in the formation of stars, planets, and larger material structures.

Symbols

molecules: chemically bound atomic systems.

stars, planets, and matter structures: larger cosmic structures assembled over time.

Meaning of the Equation

Gravity amplifies tiny initial density contrasts over cosmic time. That growth turns small early differences into galaxies, clusters, filaments, and the cosmic web.
