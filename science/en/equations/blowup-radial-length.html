Construction: Radial Core Length

Construction: Radial Core Length

Equation

$$\ell_r\asymp\tau^{1/2}$$

Literal reading

Ell subscript r is comparable to tau raised to one half.

Conceptual reading

The radial scale decreases like the square root of remaining time.

Symbols

\(\ell_r\) is characteristic core radius, with r labeling the radial direction. \(\tau\) is remaining normalized time. \(\asymp\) means upper and lower bounds by positive constant multiples, independent of tau; it does not mean equality of prefactors.

Values, units and domains

These are normalized construction variables with singular time 1. Here \(\tau=1-t>0\) is remaining time and \(0<h<1/100\) a fixed dimensionless exponent. Physical lengths, times and velocities require reference scales; a power of normalized time is not an SI dimensional equality.

Meaning

This estimates how the region of strongest flow narrows toward the singular time. It is a length scale, not the radial speed of an identified fluid parcel.

Assumptions and limits

This summarizes a leading-core estimate in the cited forced construction, not a universal flow law or an independent verification of the proof. The full result also requires the residual cancellations and smooth forcing. The similarity-defined core is not a transported material parcel.

Example

The reference function √tau is halved when tau is divided by four. Comparability says the actual core radius stays between fixed positive multiples of that function, not that each radius ratio is exactly one half.

Source

OpenAI (2026), Finite Time Blowup for Navier-Stokes, Sections 1-3.
