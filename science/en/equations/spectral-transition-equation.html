Spectral Transition Equation

Spectral Transition Equation

Equation

$$ \Delta E = h\nu = E_i - E_f $$

Literal Reading

Delta E equals h times nu, and also E subscript i minus E subscript f.

Common Reading

As written, the equation describes photon emission: the initial atomic energy exceeds the final energy.

Symbols

\(\Delta E\): the energy difference between two allowed states.

\(h\nu\): the photon energy.

\(E_i\): the initial energy level.

\(E_f\): the final energy level.

Meaning of the Equation

For emission, the photon carries \(h\nu=E_i-E_f>0\). For absorption, the atom gains energy and \(h\nu=E_f-E_i>0\). Allowed transitions between discrete levels produce characteristic spectral lines; selection rules determine which transitions can occur.
