Chemical Equilibrium Constant

Chemical Equilibrium Constant

Equation

$$K=\frac{\prod_i a_{\mathrm{products},i}^{\nu_i}}{\prod_j a_{\mathrm{reactants},j}^{\nu_j}}$$

Literal Reading

K equals the product of product activities raised to stoichiometric powers divided by the product of reactant activities raised to stoichiometric powers.

Common Reading

At equilibrium, the activities of products and reactants settle into a characteristic ratio.

Symbols

  • \(K\): equilibrium constant.
  • \(a\): activity, an effective concentration.
  • \(\nu\): stoichiometric coefficient.

Meaning of the Equation

Rigorous equilibrium constants use activities, not raw concentrations.
