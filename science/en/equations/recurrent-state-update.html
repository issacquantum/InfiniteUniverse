Recurrent State Update

Recurrent State Update

Equation

$$h_{t+1}=f(W h_t+Ux_t+b)$$

Literal Reading

h subscript t plus one equals f applied to the sum of W times h subscript t, U times x subscript t, and b.

Common Reading

This is a computational state-update model, not a literal equation of consciousness.

Symbols

  • \(h_t\): current internal state.
  • \(x_t\): current input.
  • \(W,U\): weighting matrices.
  • \(b\): bias term.
  • \(f\): nonlinear update function.

Meaning of the Equation

The hidden state acts as memory. Past inputs affect future outputs because they are folded into the state that gets updated.
