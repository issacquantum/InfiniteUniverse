ISCO (innermost stable circular orbit) Radius

Equation

$$r_{\text{ISCO}} = \frac{6GM}{c^2}$$

Literal Reading

r sub ISCO equals 6GM over c to the power of 2.

Common Reading

The innermost stable circular orbit for matter around a non rotating black hole.

Symbols

\(r_{\text{ISCO}}\): innermost stable circular orbit radius.

\(M\): black hole mass.

\(G\): gravitational constant.

\(c\): speed of light.

Meaning of the Equation

For Schwarzschild only, \(r_{\rm ISCO}=6GM/c^2\). Kerr ISCO depends on spin and on whether the orbit is prograde or retrograde.

Scope and Limits

The value \(6GM/c^2\) is the Schwarzschild result. Around rotating Kerr black holes, the innermost stable orbit depends on spin and on whether the orbit moves with or against the rotation.
