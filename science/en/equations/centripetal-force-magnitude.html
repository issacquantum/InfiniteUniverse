Centripetal Force Magnitude

Centripetal Force Magnitude

Equation

$$F=m\omega^2R$$

Literal reading

F equals m times omega squared times R.

Conceptual reading

The net inward force must supply the acceleration required for uniform circular motion.

Symbols

\(F\) is net inward force magnitude, \(m\) mass, \(\omega\) angular rate and \(R\) positive radius. The square applies to omega; all displayed quantities are scalars.

Values, units and domains

Mass is in kg, angular rate in rad/s, radius in m and force in N. Radians are dimensionless. Mass and radius are positive.

Meaning

Centripetal names the role of the net inward force, not a new interaction added to gravity, tension or friction.

Assumptions and limits

Newtonian uniform circular motion in an inertial frame. Individual forces must be combined before comparing their inward component with this requirement.

Example

For m = 1 kg, R = 2 m and omega = 3 rad/s, the required inward force is 18 N.

Source

MIT OpenCourseWare, 8.01SC Classical Mechanics (2016).
