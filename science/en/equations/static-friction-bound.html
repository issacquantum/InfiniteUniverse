Static Friction Bound

Static Friction Bound

Equation

$$|f_s|\leq\mu_s N$$

Literal reading

The absolute value of f subscript s is less than or equal to mu subscript s times N.

Conceptual reading

Static friction adjusts to prevent sliding, up to a maximum set by the contact.

Symbols

\(f_s\) is the signed tangential friction component; vertical bars take its magnitude. \(\mu_s\) is the static friction coefficient; \(N\) is the nonnegative normal-force magnitude. Subscript s means static and \(\leq\) denotes an upper bound.

Values, units and domains

Friction and normal force are in N; the coefficient is dimensionless, material- and condition-dependent. N is a force symbol here, not the unit name.

Meaning

The inequality does not set friction equal to its maximum in every stationary situation. The required tangential balance determines the actual value.

Assumptions and limits

An empirical dry-contact approximation for a contact that is not sliding. Adhesion, lubrication and complex surfaces may require a different law.

Example

With μₛ = 0.5 and normal force 20 N, the maximum is 10 N. A required balancing force of 4 N is permitted; 12 N exceeds this static bound.

Source

MIT OpenCourseWare, 8.01SC Classical Mechanics (2016).
