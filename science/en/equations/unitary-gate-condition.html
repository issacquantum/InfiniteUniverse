Unitary Gate Condition

Unitary Gate Condition

Equation

$$ U^\dagger U = I $$

Literal Reading

U dagger U equals I.

Common Reading

An ideal quantum gate preserves inner products, norms, and total probability; it can change individual amplitudes.

Symbols

\(U\): the quantum gate or circuit operation.

\(U^\dagger\): the conjugate transpose of \(U\), which reverses the operation for a unitary gate.

\(I\): the identity operator, meaning no probability is lost or gained.

Meaning of the Equation

A unitary gate is reversible and preserves total probability. That is why ideal closed-system quantum circuits evolve amplitudes without losing normalization.
