Heat Stability Condition

Heat Stability Condition

Equation

$$ \lambda=\alpha\frac{\Delta t}{\Delta x^2}\le\frac{1}{2}\quad\text{(1D FTCS)} $$
$$ \lambda_x+\lambda_y\le\frac{1}{2},\qquad \lambda_x=\lambda_y=\lambda\Rightarrow \lambda\le\frac{1}{4} $$

Literal Reading

For one-dimensional FTCS, lambda equals alpha times delta t over delta x squared and is at most one half. In two dimensions, lambda subscript x plus lambda subscript y is at most one half; if both equal lambda, lambda is at most one quarter.

Common Reading

The allowed time step gets smaller when heat can spread in more directions.

Symbols

\(\lambda\): diffusion number for one direction.

\(\lambda_x,\lambda_y\): diffusion numbers in the horizontal and vertical grid directions.

\(\alpha\): thermal diffusivity.

\(\Delta t\): time step.

\(\Delta x\): grid spacing.

Meaning of the Equation

This stability condition is for explicit FTCS heat-equation discretization, not for all heat-equation solvers.
